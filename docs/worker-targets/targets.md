# Targets of the Tau Ceti workers of GLCinLean
<!-- tauceti-targets:v1 -->
Goal: the definitions that the statement of the de Rham geometric Langlands conjecture ([GLC1] Conj. 1.6.7) needs and that a Tau Ceti roadmap owns. Each item is one pull request: one complete statement with its API and the lemmas named for it, at most 3,000 added lines and 1,250 lines per new file. A corollary or a check of an item goes into the pull request of that item, not into its own. Tau Ceti allows no stacked pull request: build only on Tau Ceti main. Search Tau Ceti main and the open pull requests on GitHub and Forgejo before you write, and write nothing that one of them has. The clause `not:` names open pull requests that must not be repeated, `source:` the passage of the roadmap and the reference, `serves:` the definition that needs the item. When the item cannot be written as stated, end the round without a pull request and say why.

- [ ] `essentially-surjective-trivial-fibration` — A categorical fibration `SSet.CategoricalFibration` between quasicategories that is fully faithful `SSet.IsFullyFaithful` and essentially surjective is a trivial fibration, and a trivial fibration between quasicategories is a categorical equivalence `SSet.QCat.IsCategoricalEquivalence`. (serves: Infinity.mappingSpaces, Infinity.linearEquivalence; needs: `essentially-surjective-trivial-fibration`, `categorical-equivalence-fully-faithful`; source: G2.11, passages Categorical model and functor spaces and Unmarked straightening and the categorical model)
- [ ] `limit-mapping-space-criterion` — The mapping-space criterion for limits. A cone over `p : K ⟶ C` with apex `x` is a limit cone `SSet.slice.IsLimit` exactly when, for every vertex `y`, composition with the cone is a weak homotopy equivalence from the right mapping space `SSet.rightMappingSpace C y x` to the fibre over `y` of the slice projection from `C_{/p}` to `C`. The dual statement for colimit cocones `SSet.coslice.IsColimit`. (serves: Infinity.limits; needs: `rightfibration-kan-complex-trivial-fibration`; source: G2.11, passage Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements, HTT Lemma 4.2.4.3)
- [ ] `small-limits-and-colimits` — Small limits and colimits of a quasicategory and their preservation. For a quasicategory `C` and a universe `w`: `C` has limits, respectively colimits, indexed by `w`-small simplicial sets when every diagram in `C` indexed by the universe lift (`SSet.uliftFunctor`) of a simplicial set of the universe `w` has a limit cone `SSet.slice.IsLimit`, respectively a colimit cocone `SSet.coslice.IsColimit`. A map `F : C ⟶ D` of quasicategories preserves these colimits when the cocone map `SSet.coslice.map` sends each such colimit cocone to a colimit cocone; the same for limits with `SSet.slice.map`. With identities, composites, and the duality of the two notions through `SSet.coslice.isColimit_iff_isLimit_op`. Check: `Δ[0]` has all small limits and colimits. The `κ`-filtered colimits are the item `kappa-filtered-colimits` of EnhancedDerivedSheaves (open #46129): do not repeat them. (serves: Infinity.limits, Infinity.presentableCategory, Infinity.continuousLinearFunctor; needs: none; source: G2.11, passage Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements, HTT Section 1.2.13)
- [ ] `right-kan-extension` — Right Kan extensions along a map of simplicial sets into a quasicategory, defined by pointwise limit cones over the slices. - [ ] `right-kan-extension-restriction` — The functor-space universal property of right Kan extensions. For a full subcategory `C₀` of `C` and a quasicategory `D`, the restriction from the functors `C ⟶ D` that are right Kan extensions of their restriction to `C₀`, to the functors `C₀ ⟶ D` that have one, is a trivial fibration. (serves: Infinity.rightKanExtension; needs: `right-kan-extension`, `categorical-equivalence-criterion`; source: G2.11, passage Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements, HTT Section 4.3.2)
- [ ] `quasicategory-localization` — The localization of a simplicial set at a set of edges, defined by its functor-space universal property. For a simplicial set `C`, a set `W` of edges of `C`, a quasicategory `D` and a map `f : C ⟶ D`: `f` is a localization at `W` when it sends every edge of `W` to an equivalence edge `SSet.Edge.IsEquivalence` and, for every quasicategory `E` of the universe of `C` and `D`, precomposition with `f` is a categorical equivalence `SSet.QCat.IsCategoricalEquivalence` from the functor quasicategory `(ihom D).obj E` to the full subquasicategory `SSet.fullEnhancedSubcategory` of `(ihom C).obj E` on the maps that send `W` to equivalence edges. Prove: for a categorical equivalence `g` from `D` to a quasicategory, `f` is a localization at `W` exactly when `f ≫ g` is; a map from `C` to `D` that is joined to a localization at `W` by an equivalence edge of the functor quasicategory `(ihom C).obj D` is one; the induced functor of homotopy categories `SSet.mapHomotopyCategory f` is a localization of categories at the classes of `W` (Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Functor.IsLocalization`); and the identity of a quasicategory is a localization at its equivalence edges. Checks: the map from `Δ[1]` to `Δ[0]` is a localization at the nondegenerate edge of `Δ[1]`, and it is not a localization at the empty set of edges. (serves: Infinity.IsLocalization; needs: none; source: G2.11, passage Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements, the relative localization with its functor-space universal property, which EnhancedDerivedSheaves E0.3 cites through BordismFormalGroups F6.5, HA Definition 1.3.4.1 and HTT Section 1.2.7)
- [ ] `quasicategory-localization-existence` — Existence and uniqueness of the localization of `quasicategory-localization`. Existence: for every simplicial set `C` and every set `W` of edges of `C` there are a quasicategory `D` and a map `f : C ⟶ D` that is a localization of `C` at `W`. Relative form: when a map `p : C ⟶ B` to a quasicategory sends every edge of `W` to a degenerate edge, `D` and `f` can be chosen with an inner fibration `q : D ⟶ B` such that `f ≫ q = p`. Uniqueness: for two localizations `f : C ⟶ D` and `f' : C ⟶ D'` at `W` there is a categorical equivalence `g : D ⟶ D'` such that `f ≫ g` and `f'` are joined by an equivalence edge of the functor quasicategory `(ihom C).obj D'`. The construction of the roadmap: glue along each edge of `W` a copy of the nerve of the category with two objects and one isomorphism between them, then factor the map to `B` as an inner anodyne map followed by an inner fibration. Check: for a quasicategory `C` and `W` empty, every localization of `C` at `W` is a categorical equivalence. (serves: Infinity.IsLocalization; needs: `quasicategory-localization`; source: G2.11, passage Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements, the sentences Construct relative localization by the categorical homotopy pushout replacing the marked edges by walking equivalences and Prove its functor-space universal property, HTT Section 1.2.7)
