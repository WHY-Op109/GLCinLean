# Targets of the Tau Ceti workers of GLCinLean

Goal: the definitions that the statement of the de Rham geometric Langlands conjecture ([GLC1] Conj. 1.6.7) needs and that a Tau Ceti roadmap owns. Each item is one pull request: one complete statement with its API and the lemmas named for it, at most 3,000 added lines and 1,250 lines per new file. A corollary or a check of an item goes into the pull request of that item, not into its own. Tau Ceti allows no stacked pull request: build only on Tau Ceti main. Search Tau Ceti main and the open pull requests on GitHub and Forgejo before you write, and write nothing that one of them has. The clause `not:` names open pull requests that must not be repeated, `source:` the passage of the roadmap and the reference, `serves:` the definition that needs the item. When the item cannot be written as stated, end the round without a pull request and say why.

## AlgebraicTopology
- [ ] `simplicial-subdivision-realization-standard-simplex` — Identify Mathlib's `SSet.toTop` realization of the nerve of a partial order with the realization of Tau Ceti's `AbstractSimplicialComplex.orderComplex`. On each simplex given by a monotone chain, push its weights to the vertices, adding the weights at repetitions. Construct the inverse by the increasing enumeration of the finite support using `SSet.toTopObjMk` and check continuity on each face by `continuous_iff_faceInclusion`. Prove that the comparison is natural under monotone maps. Apply `SSet.stdSimplex.sdIso` and the existing abstract barycentric subdivision homeomorphism to identify the realization of `SSet.sd` of a standard simplex with its topological standard simplex. Give the barycentre formula, its naturality for injective simplex operators and the restriction to faces. Check that the full edge of Δ[1] gives the midpoint in the subdivision. This supplies the finite-simplex comparison for relative simplicial approximation. (serves: Infinity.mappingSpaces, Infinity.linearEquivalence; needs: none; source: AlgebraicTopology §8.2 compatible finite-simplex restrictions and subdivision; not: GitHub #41049, GitHub #43073, GitHub #47294)
- [ ] `simplicial-set-subdivision-last-vertex` — Construct the natural transformation `SSet.sd ⟶ 𝟭 SSet` on Mathlib's subdivision of simplicial sets. On the subdivision of each standard simplex the map is induced from the monotone map sending a nonempty face to its largest vertex. Use `SSet.stdSimplex.isoNerve`, `SSet.stdSimplex.sdIso`, and the left Kan extension defining `SSet.sd`. Give the simplex computation, compatibility with the face and degeneracy maps and the restriction squares for subcomplex inclusions, also on iteration. Check that the new vertex of the subdivided interval goes to the last vertex while both original endpoints are fixed. Tau Ceti's `AbstractSimplicialComplex.lastVertexMap` concerns ordered abstract complexes. Use it where the carriers agree. This is the subdivision input to relative simplicial approximation in §8.2. (serves: Infinity.mappingSpaces, Infinity.linearEquivalence; needs: none; source: AlgebraicTopology §8.2 and the Mathlib subdivision construction citing Jardine Simplicial approximation)
- [ ] `relative-simplicial-approximation` — Prove the native simplicial-set relative approximation used by the Kan realization-unit theorem. Let A ↪ L be a finite simplicial pair, K a Kan complex, a : A → K a simplicial map and f : |L| → |K| a continuous map restricting to |a|. After iterated subdivision of L, give a simplicial map restricting on the subdivided A to a composed with its iterated last-vertex map. Compare its realization with f composed with the realization of the iterated last-vertex map, by a homotopy fixed on the realization of the subdivided A. Use the finite-simplex barycentric comparison and keep track of its homotopy to the last-vertex realization. Include the relative homotopy comparison for pointed simplices and their constant boundaries, so iterated last-vertex precomposition identifies the corresponding pointed relative simplicial homotopy classes. Reuse the ordered-complex approximation and realization API where applicable. (serves: Infinity.mappingSpaces, Infinity.linearEquivalence; needs: `simplicial-set-subdivision-last-vertex`, `simplicial-subdivision-realization-standard-simplex`; source: AlgebraicTopology §8.2, relative simplicial approximation for the realization unit)
- [ ] `kan-realization-unit-homotopy-iso` — For a Kan complex K and a vertex x, prove that Mathlib's actual `sSetTopAdj.unit.app K` induces an isomorphism on the based Kan homotopy group `SSet.KanComplex.π` in every positive degree. Use the relative approximation for representatives and homotopies fixed on the boundary. The component comparison is already `SSet.bijective_mapπ₀_unit`. Use the existing Kan-to-cubical comparison with its product convention.
