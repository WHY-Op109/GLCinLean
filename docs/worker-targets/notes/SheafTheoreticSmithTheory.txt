The targets are in Section 3.0 "The loop Grassmannian over an arbitrary field" and Section 6.2 "Semi-infinite intersections at extreme weights": the loop Grassmannian LG/L+G of a split connected reductive group over a field, its Schubert varieties, the affine flag variety with the Iwahori orbits and the Demazure varieties, the convolution Grassmannian and the semi-infinite orbits. The Smith theory, the Tate categories, the etale sheaves and the tilting modules of the other layers are not targets. State each result for every commutative ring or every field for which the proof works, keep nonreduced test rings, and prove nothing that is special to characteristic p.
