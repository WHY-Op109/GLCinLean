The roadmap AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry (Layer 4) provides banded gerbes on a site with an abelian band, their restriction to a slice site, relative gerbes, the classifying gerbe and neutral gerbes, the change of band, the product, contracted sum and inverse on one site, the root gerbe of an invertible sheaf on a scheme, and the groupoid of n-th roots of an invertible sheaf (`LineBundle.Root`, for every natural number n, with no hypothesis). Four of these are prerequisites of targets and not named targets: the groupoid of roots, the isomorphism `f^*(M ⊗ N) ≅ f^*M ⊗ f^*N` of invertible sheaves (`LineBundle.pullbackTensorIso`), the restriction to a slice, and the contracted sum. If Tau Ceti's review refuses one of them as outside the roadmap, that node comes back through `prompts/change.md`. SchemeAndStackFoundations provides stackification and 2-fibre products on a site (`Stacks.stackification`, split from `Stacks.algebraicStack`), the site-level quotient stack, the Kummer sequence on the big étale site for an invertible exponent, and the Picard groupoid of a scheme. The site is the big étale site (schemes over a scheme, étale topology), so pullback of a gerbe is restriction to a slice. Written here, because no roadmap target states or needs them: gerbes on a prestack as a limit over test schemes, the coherence of the tensor product, torsors and trivializations on a prestack (`Stacks.EtaleGerbe`); the root gerbe on a prestack with the formulas for `R_n(L ⊗ M)` and `R_n(L^{-1})` (`Stacks.rootGerbe`); the tensor product, dual and twist of roots (`LineBundle.Root.tensorProduct`); and the monoidal structure of the n-th tensor power functor of a braided monoidal category (`CategoryTheory.tensorPowFunctor`). The band of `Stacks.EtaleGerbe` is a sheaf of abelian groups on the big étale site of the base field; root gerbes and the twists use μ_n (the owner's decision of 2026-11-13). - **The settings of the Tau Ceti workers** (the owner, 2026-11-19). Model and effort are the Worker's defaults: Opus 5.5 at high effort for the author workers, and `agent = "auto"` for the maintenance worker. The pace of all six is linear, `pace = "0:0,100:100"`: a worker runs while the usage of the week is below the elapsed share of the week. `ignore_quota` is not used; it is the next step for chosen workers if the linear pace proves too slow. The author workers work only on targets of the plan: the guidance of each names them and tells the worker to end a round without a pull request when none is available, because Tau Ceti's rules would otherwise send it to another target of the roadmap. The session compares each new pull request of a worker with the plan. The maintenance worker keeps the phases `review`, `overlap-fix` and `overlap-scan`: in Tau Ceti the contributors' workers run the reviews, a pull request merges only when it is reviewed, and Tau Ceti discourages workers that only write. The session does not plan its own work by the weekly usage of the subscriptions, and it reports the usage only when a worker stops on a limit of the provider.
- **Generality** (2026-11-11 and 2026-11-13). A definition gets its natural generality. A theorem stated more narrowly than its source is under-generalization. A theorem that serves only a proof is stated under the hypotheses its proof uses, with the case used as an instance.
- **The ∞-categorical layer** (2026-11-10). Build the enhanced theory on Mathlib quasicategories and Tau Ceti's DG nerve and K-injective models, following the accepted targets of EnhancedDerivedSheaves and of GeneralizedCohomology G2.11. - **Open Tau Ceti roadmap pull requests** (2026-11-10). Use them as design evidence, with their exact head recorded. The accepted roadmap stays the authority until a proposal merges. Never import a proposal's `Suggested.lean` or pin this project to an unmerged branch.
- **Other Lean libraries** (2026-11-10). Read-only discovery and reuse of ideas under compatible licences. Adding a dependency first needs a list of the declarations used, compatible Lean and Mathlib versions, a licence check, an axiom audit and a comparison with the existing objects. AxiomMath Stacks and Weil are prior art for Worker ports, case by case.
- **Names** (2026-11-13). Category-, scheme- and prestack-valued constructions and predicates get UpperCamelCase names, as the statement's `QCohLS`, `DmodHalf` and `RepRan` do. A node that carries data a later definition uses is a definition. Roadmap-owned nodes keep their ids, which Tau Ceti fixes.
