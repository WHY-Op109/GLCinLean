# Start

**Goal.** Do step 3 of the workflow of `AGENTS.md`: bring the work in line with the plan, and write what is ready.

**Done when**
- the Tau Ceti workers are in line with the ready roadmap modules: started, retargeted or stopped, as many as the plan and the quota allow;
- each merged Tau Ceti pull request that supplies a plan node is folded in ("Plan" in `AGENTS.md`);
- each ready module here is written, checked with `prompts/check.md` and pushed, or its line of work is with `prompts/change.md`;
- `STATUS.md` records the state.
