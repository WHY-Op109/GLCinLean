# Blueprint of the de Rham statement

This plan covers the statement of `GLCinLean/Statement/`: [GLC1] Conj. 1.6.7, with the existence part of Cor. 1.6.5, in the form with the Hecke action. The target is any algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, a smooth proper connected curve, a pinned reductive group, and a chosen theta characteristic. The plan holds every construction that the statement uses, the examples that rule out a trivial reading of its placeholders, and the comparison results for its definitions (independence of the pinning, the half twist). The statement uses the Hecke action of `Rep(Ǧ)_Ran` and the spectral localization functor `Loc`, but no action of `QCoh(LS_Ǧ)`, no coarse functor and no `𝕃_G`. So the spectral decomposition and its proof (vanishing, Eisenstein series, the critical level), the coarse functor, and the comparison of the statement with the form of [GLC1] Cor. 1.6.5 are not in this plan. They are in `plans-proof/`, the plan of the proof of the main theorem. The Betti variants are not planned.

## Reading the plan

Every TOML file is a tracker module plan. Every node records a paper locator (`source`), what the object is and who provides it (`desc`), and its immediate prerequisites (`deps`).

- `plans/GLCinLean/` holds the nodes written here, in subject folders as in Mathlib; the statement has its own `Statement` folder. Their `desc` says "Provider: here", and names the roadmap targets that the construction uses.
- `plans/Roadmap/<area>/` holds the nodes that a Tau Ceti roadmap owns, with their ids unchanged. `<area>` is the roadmap's folder, which is also the Worker's `--roadmap-only` value. Their `desc` says "Provider: roadmap ⟨area⟩, ⟨target⟩", where the target is a section title or a Lean name of the roadmap. A node here may depend on a roadmap node, never the other way round.
- `plans-proof/` holds the nodes that only the proof of the main theorem needs, in the same layout (`plans-proof/GLCinLean/`, `plans-proof/Roadmap/<area>/`); `plans-proof/README.md` says how it is used. The tracker does not read it. A node in `plans/` never depends on a node in `plans-proof/`; lint reports such a dependency as unknown.
- An existing Mathlib or Tau Ceti declaration is not a node. The node that uses one names it and its import module in its `desc`, under "Upstream declarations used".

When a roadmap node lands upstream, it is deleted only when its declaration is at this project's pin, its interface is checked, and a fresh agent has reviewed its meaning with `prompts/review.md`. The nodes that use it are updated in the same commit.

On declarations that already exist (the statement's placeholders), `desc` and `deps` are kept although `lake exe tracker lint` reports them as superseded; `kind` is not kept, because Lean gives it. The reason: a placeholder has no real dependencies in Lean, so its suggested `deps` are the only link from the statement to the rest of the plan, and its `desc` records how it is to be replaced. Lean and its docstrings remain authoritative for the compiled object.

The one upstream snapshot is in `docs/blueprint-providers.md`. `STATUS.md` holds the current state, the decisions and the nodes that are held back.

## Construction chain

1. Existing fields, schemes, reductive Hopf algebras, pinnings, invertible sheaves and differentials supply the geometric input. Root-datum extraction/classification and dual group existence belong to the accepted reductive-group roadmaps.
2. Existing quasicategories, mapping spaces, strict DG categories and DG nerves are starting points. Presentability, stable k-linearity, coherent tensor/module structures, adjunctions, compactness, Ind-completion and t-structures are separate enhanced obligations.
3. Derived affine schemes and prestacks supply de Rham prestacks, derived mapping stacks, QCoh, IndCoh and singular support. LS is Maps(X_dR, B dualG), retaining its derived structure.
4. The spectral support is the closed cone of horizontal nilpotent **coadjoint** sections in Sing(LS), containing zero. It is not the automorphic nilpotent cone in T*Bun.
5. The automorphic category is Dmod(Bun_G) twisted by the square-root gerbe of the normalized determinant line. The theta characteristic trivializes that gerbe and fixes the Whittaker normalization. The integral cocharacter 2ρ is applied to its frame torsor.
6. The spherical perverse category, convolution, fusion and Tannakian reconstruction supply the geometric Satake heart equivalence. Its de Rham factorization extension, compatible with the chosen duality and half twist, supplies the Hecke action of `Rep(Ǧ)_Ran` on the automorphic category. A change of the pinning of the Tannakian group changes the Hecke action only up to isomorphism. The spectral localization `Loc : Rep(Ǧ)_Ran → QCoh(LS_Ǧ)` is pullback along the evaluation map `LS_Ǧ × Ran → (BǦ)_Ran` followed by pushforward along `Ran`; its monoidal structure, its fully faithful right adjoint and the spectral decomposition are in the plan of the proof. The vacuum Poincaré object is `p_! χ^*(exp)`, with the proof that `p_!` is defined on it.
7. Coarsening is the continuous functor `Ψ_Nilp,{0} : IndCoh_Nilp(LS_Ǧ) → QCoh(LS_Ǧ)`. The coarse functor, the right adjoint of acting on the Poincaré object, is in the plan of the proof.
8. The main theorem asserts existence of a continuous functor with the two properties, and equivalence of every such functor. Its second property uses no coarse functor: it is one equivalence `Maps(V ⋆ Poinc, M) ≃ Maps(Loc V, Ψ (F M))` of mapping spaces, natural in `V` in `Rep(Ǧ)_Ran` and `M` together, with the Hecke action and `Loc` of step 6. By [GLC1] Thm. 1.2.4 it is `Ψ ∘ F ≃ 𝕃_{G,coarse}` in an equivalent form for the underlying functors of ∞-categories. The comparison theorem `Langlands.isLanglandsFunctor_iff_comp_psi_iso_coarse` and the uniqueness theorem `Langlands.isEquivalence_iff_of_isLanglandsFunctor` are obligations of the proof, in `plans-proof/`. A separately chosen functor depending on an admitted existence theorem is not part of the statement.
