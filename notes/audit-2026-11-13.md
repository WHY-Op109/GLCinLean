## Findings, ranked by their effect on the goal

### 1. Two JacobianChallenge nodes already exist in Tau Ceti (high)

`plans/Roadmap/JacobianChallenge/Jacobian.toml` plans `Curves.picard_degree`: "the degree deg : Pic X → ℤ …, a group homomorphism with deg 𝒪(x) = 1 for a rational point x, and Pic⁰ X = ker deg". The pinned Tau Ceti has all three parts:

- `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.eulerDegreeHom : Additive (LineBundleClass X) →+ ℤ` and `LineBundleClass.picZero` (its kernel), in `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/PicZero.lean`;
- `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.eulerDegree_toLineBundleClass_ofPoint_of_section` (deg 𝒪_X(x₀) = 1), in `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RationalPoint/Degree.lean`.

`plans/Roadmap/JacobianChallenge/SerreDuality.toml` plans `Curves.degree_canonical`: deg ω_X = 2g − 2. The pinned Tau Ceti has `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.eulerDegree_relativeDifferentials` in `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/Canonical.lean`.

Both are stated for a proper integral curve with discrete valuation rings in codimension one and finite-dimensional `H¹(X, 𝒪_X)`; the second also needs an exact constant field and the valuative existence criterion. A smooth proper geometrically connected curve satisfies all of them. `LineBundleClass.eulerDegree_mul_of_smooth` (pin, `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Degree.lean`) shows how the first set follows from smoothness, in a few lines. No Tau Ceti theorem states the smooth case of `eulerDegree_relativeDifferentials`. JacobianChallenge's own `STATUS.md` (2026-11-12) says "Layer A is done for curves, and the curve case of Layer B is in place".

These files were at the pin on 2026-11-10 too, so the provider check of that day missed them. `AGENTS.md` line 31 forbids a second copy, and `STATUS.md` ("Plan") says existing declarations are not nodes. The Worker line for JacobianChallenge in `STATUS.md` asks for both; a Worker round would duplicate Tau Ceti code.

What stays open in JacobianChallenge: `Curves.serreTrace` (Tau Ceti has only a noncanonical duality, "not a natural trace pairing"), Layer D (`Curves.picardZero_isAbelianVariety`; nothing is representable yet) and Layer E (`Curves.abelianVariety_multiplication_surjective`; no `[n]` for `n ≠ 0`).

### 2. `Constructible.ComplexPoints` is owned by an accepted roadmap, not only by #848 (high)

`STATUS.md`, "Held", holds `Constructible.ComplexPoints` because "open roadmap PR #848 would own it". #1676, merged on 2026-11-10 after the provider check commit `651d0822`, added ReductiveGroups Layer 3A.5. Its text (README at `7342e8fd`, "3A.5 Properness and the complex comparison") says: "This section is the single owner of complex points, their classical topology, the smooth charts of smooth ℂ-schemes and the complex Lie-group structure on G(ℂ)". Its scope section says "ComplexComparisonPartII C-1.1 … for 3A.5c–3A.5e it is a consumer".

3A.5b–3A.5c match the node item for item. The node asks for Y(ℂ) of a scheme locally of finite type over ℂ with the classical topology, functorial, locally compact, Hausdorff when Y is separated (with the doubled line as the counterexample), open and closed embeddings, and proper maps to proper maps. 3A.5c gives the classical topology for every ℂ-scheme locally of finite type, from the pinned `TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.topology` (`TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme.lean`). Through 3A.5b it gives open-embedding charts, the functor to spaces, open and closed embeddings, Hausdorff points for separated X and local compactness. Its API gives proper maps (`IsProperMap`) and the same doubled-line check.

So the reason for holding the node is gone, and `AGENTS.md` line 12 sends it to Tau Ceti. 24 nodes here depend on it, among them `Constructible.DerivedCategory`, which is not held. The owner chose the complex-analytic route to parity on 2026-11-10 (Q1), so the node is needed. Weil's `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ComplexPoints` stays prior art for the Worker.

### 4. The ReductiveGroupsPartII check of "Held" can be closed (medium)

It can move to `plans/Roadmap/ReductiveGroupsPartII/` as a prerequisite of that item, or stay here. I recommend the move, because the general statement is what the roadmap item rests on, and the Worker may contribute a genuine prerequisite of a target.

### 5. The non-unital variant added in `edaeafd` has no upstream owner (medium)

`edaeafd` (after `1b60708`) adds to `Infinity.monoidalCategory` "the non-unital variant, with no unit object, which Rep(Ǧ)_Ran needs", and to `Infinity.moduleCategory` the modules over it. Both nodes are in `plans/Roadmap/BordismFormalGroups/` and name BordismFormalGroups and EnhancedDerivedSheaves as providers. Neither README mentions non-unital monoidal categories, and neither does #1901 (HigherToposTheory). The cited EnhancedDerivedSheaves lane is "Symmetric monoidal ∞-categories and ∞-operads"; a non-unital monoidal category is an algebra over the non-unital associative ∞-operad, so the lane may give it, but it does not say so. The node should say which part is the roadmap's and which is written here, or the variant should be its own node here. This is for the next review of the plan, together with the open item "Which monoidal structure of Rep(Ǧ)_Ran".

### 6. The theta-characteristic example does not need the Jacobian (medium)

`Curves.nonempty_thetaCharacteristic` waits for JacobianChallenge Layers D and E, which have no Jacobian scheme yet. In the statement's plan its only user is `Examples.exists_setting_rank_pos`, whose node says to construct the setting "on P¹". On ℙ¹ the theta characteristic is 𝒪(−1). The pin has `AlgebraicGeometry.Proj.twistingSheaf`. Tau Ceti main has `projectiveLine.nonempty_iso_twistingSheaf_iff` (a line bundle on ℙ¹ is 𝒪(n) exactly when its degree is n), which with `eulerDegree_relativeDifferentials` gives ω_{ℙ¹} ≅ 𝒪(−2). If the example depends on a ℙ¹ theta characteristic instead of the general theorem, it no longer waits for the Jacobian. The general theorem stays in the plan as the check that the standing hypotheses are not vacuous for any curve.
