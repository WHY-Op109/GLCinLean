# Upstream check, 2026-11-10

This note records what changed outside this project since the snapshots in `docs/blueprint-providers.md`, and what that means for the plan in `plans/`. It only reads upstream: no pull request, issue or comment was opened, and no pin was moved. Plan node ids below omit the `GLCinLean.` prefix.

## Key points

1. Nothing merged since the snapshots supplies a plan node. All 39 Tau Ceti leaves of the plan still exist on Tau Ceti main under the same name and module, and no leaf module changed.
2. Three open roadmap pull requests would, if accepted, give an owner to nodes that the plan now marks "Missing; implement here":
   - #1734 (SheafTheoreticSmithTheory §3.0) claims the loop Grassmannian of a split reductive group over any field, with its Schubert orbits, affine flags, Demazure resolutions, convolution, Beilinson–Drinfeld Grassmannians over 𝔸^J and semi-infinite orbits. Nine plan nodes are affected.
   - #1687 (AnalyticStacks Layer 1) claims quasi-coherent sheaves on derived affine schemes, their extension to stacks, formal completions and QCoh(BG). This is the construction the plan marks as missing for `Sheaves.qcoh`.
   - #848 (ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1) claims scheme analytification, which covers `Constructible.complexPoints`. While these are open, the nodes stay as they are. If one is accepted, its nodes become roadmap-owned and go through Tau Ceti Worker, not this repository.
3. Two providers that already exist on roadmap main are missing from the provider record: PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras §5.1 (determinant lines of perfect complexes over any commutative ring), for `Sheaves.determinant`, and SchubertGeometry (cells of finite flag varieties), for `Hecke.schubertStratum_deRhamCohomology_even`.
4. AxiomMath Stacks has Lean proofs of several classical inputs: Stacks 03RM (vanishing of H^q(X_et, 𝐆_m) for q ≥ 2 on a smooth curve over an algebraically closed field), algebraic stacks, the cotangent complex of a ring map, a two-term determinant functor, algebraic de Rham cohomology and gerbes. AxiomMath Weil defines the complex points Y(ℂ) with the analytic topology. None can be imported directly. Both are on older Lean and Mathlib versions, and the project rules allow only Mathlib and Tau Ceti as sources. They are useful as prior art for a port into Tau Ceti or for proof routes.

### 3.2 Would make a roadmap the owner of plan nodes

All three have the labels `awaiting-review` and `awaiting-dependencies`, and none is approved.

- **#1734 SheafTheoreticSmithTheory**, head `918421742b2f159a00663d66272a7206a62b2280`, §3.0 "The loop Grassmannian over an arbitrary field". For a split connected reductive group over any field F, the PR specifies the following:
  - LH, L⁺H and the jet groups LⁿH;
  - Gr_H as an fpqc quotient that represents torsors with a trivialization, with Beauville–Laszlo gluing and ind-projectivity;
  - the L⁺H-orbits Gr^λ (dimension ⟨2ρ,λ⟩, connected stabilizers, closure order) and the components indexed by π₁;
  - Fl_H, its Iwahori orbits indexed by W̃, and Demazure resolutions;
  - the convolution Grassmannian;
  - Beilinson–Drinfeld Grassmannians over 𝔸^J with factorization;
  - the semi-infinite orbits S_ν and T_ν as attractors and repellers.

  Its §6.2 gives the dimensions of the semi-infinite intersections. The geometry is over 𝔸^J, not over the powers of a curve X; the PR says it does not extend its §§1.1–1.3 to other base fields. **Affects (owner):** `Hecke.loopArcGroups`, `Hecke.affineGrassmannian`, `Hecke.affineGrassmannian_reducedComponents`, `Hecke.schubertStratification`, `Hecke.iwahoriWeylGroup`, `Hecke.bottSamelsonResolution` (as Demazure varieties), `Satake.semiInfiniteOrbits`, `Satake.semiInfiniteIntersection_dimension`, and the geometric part of `Satake.convolution`. **Partly:** `Satake.fusion` and `Hecke.correspondence` (over 𝔸^J, not over X^I or Ran).
- **#1687 AnalyticStacks**, head `8b3b4cb179817b67d536ccb7c09aa509bce312cc`.
  - Layer 0: abstract three- and six-functor formalisms on ∞-categories (§0.6 extends a formalism from a site to its sheaves).
  - Layer 1: quasi-coherent six functors on algebraic stacks. §1.1 sets D(R) = Mod_R(D(ℤ)) on derived affine schemes (animated rings). §1.2 covers the !-topology and algebraic stacks. §1.4 covers formal completions. §1.5 covers BG for a flat affine group, with D(BG) as comodules and its t-structure.
  - It takes the higher-category foundations from EnhancedDerivedSheaves (E0.3–E0.8, E3, E5).
  - It works over essentially small sites, in the !-topology.

  **Affects (owner, to be checked against its scope):** `Sheaves.qcoh` and `Sheaves.qcohGlobalSections`. **Partly:** `Derived.formalCompletion` (defined there by a topological condition, not as Y ×_{Y_dR} Z_dR) and `LocalSystems.classifyingStack` (QCoh(BG)). Before relying on it, check two things: that its D* on stacks is the plan's right Kan extension along all affine maps, and that LS_G and Bun_G fall in its class of stacks.

## 6. Providers on roadmap main that the record does not list

These existed at the recorded snapshot, so they are not changes. They were found during this check:

- **PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras §5.1 "Determinant lines of perfect complexes".** It covers any commutative ring, with `detLine`, `detLine_quasiIso`, `detLine_shortExact`, `detLine_baseChange`, `detLine_dual`, `detLine_shift` and `detLine_acyclic`, after Knudsen–Mumford.
