## Main result

The plan still reaches a fully defined statement, and the statement is unchanged. What changed is the ownership of the work. In five days about 200 roadmaps merged into TauCetiRoadmap. Several of them state as targets objects that the plan writes here: banded gerbes and root gerbes, the coadjoint quotient, the decomposition theorem over ℂ, D-modules on smooth schemes, formal loop groups, Tannakian reconstruction, and QCoh on prestacks of ordinary rings. Other contributors have open Tau Ceti pull requests on three of the seven targets.

### Big changes (for `prompts/change.md`)

**B1. Gerbes and roots move to AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry Layer 4.** Nodes: `Stacks.EtaleGerbe`, `Stacks.rootGerbe`, `LineBundle.Root`. Evidence: the roadmap merged on 2026-11-15 (#853, head `e5f9c4c7`); its Layer 4 is in `TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry/README.md`, §§4.1–4.3.

- Names and conventions of the merged roadmap. A gerbe is a `Cat`-valued pseudofunctor `F` on a site `(C, J)` with `IsGerbe F J`: Mathlib's `Pseudofunctor.IsStack`, groupoid fibres, locally nonempty, locally connected (§4.1). A band is a sheaf `A : Sheaf J AddCommGrpCat`, and `AbelianBanding` gives compatible isomorphisms `A(U) ≅ Aut(x)`. Morphisms are `BandPreserving` strong transformations, with modifications between them; `band_morphism_equivalence` says each is an equivalence (§4.2). §4.3 has `classifyingAbelianGerbe`, `Neutralization`, `neutralizationEquivalence`, `neutralSelfEquivalences`, `changeBand`, `liftingGerbe`, `h2Classification` and `classSitePullback`. The root gerbe is `rootGerbe`: for a scheme `X`, an invertible sheaf `L` and `n > 0` invertible on `X`, objects over `T` are pairs `(M, φ : M^{⊗n} ≅ L|_T)`; it is defined as the lifting gerbe of the Kummer sequence. `rootGerbeClass` uses the small étale site of `X`. `rootO1Nonneutral` is the example on `ℙ¹`.
- These agree with the two nodes: the carrier (`Pseudofunctor.IsStack` on the small étale site), the band `μ_n`, the hypothesis "n invertible on the base", and "not the root stack of a line bundle with a section".
- What moves. From `Stacks.EtaleGerbe`: the notion of a `μ_n`-gerbe on the étale site of a scheme, band-preserving equivalences and the isomorphisms between them, trivializations (`Neutralization`), the torsor by which two trivializations differ (`neutralSelfEquivalences`), local triviality, and the class in `H²`. From `Stacks.rootGerbe`: the root gerbe of a line bundle on a scheme, the proof that it is a gerbe (the Kummer step), its band, its base change, and the `ℙ¹` example.
- What stays here. Gerbes on a prestack as a point of the limit over test schemes, pullback along maps of prestacks, the tensor product and the inverse of gerbes (no roadmap target states them; they can be built from `changeBand`), the symmetric monoidal 2-groupoid, the formulas `R_n(L ⊗ M)` and `R_n(L^{-1})`, the root gerbe on a prestack, and the example on `𝔾_m`.
- `LineBundle.Root`, which is ready now, is the fibre groupoid of the roadmap's `rootGerbe`: the same objects, the same arrows and the same pullback. It also needs specified isomorphisms for `tensorPow` (item 6 below), which are improvements of existing Tau Ceti code. Proposal: it moves with the two gerbe nodes, as a prerequisite of the target `rootGerbe`, to `plans/Roadmap/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry/`, and an author worker pinned to that area writes it together with the specified isomorphisms of `tensorPow`. If it is written here instead, the exponent-law isomorphisms become a second API beside `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundle.Power`. Until this is decided, a writer should not start `GLCinLean.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundle.Root`.
- After the move, the owner's decision of 2026-11-13 applies as recorded: the core definition takes an abelian band, and root gerbes and the twists use `μ_n`.
