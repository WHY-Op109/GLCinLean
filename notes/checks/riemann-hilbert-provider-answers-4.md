## The open item, for "Open items" of `STATUS.md`

**The provider and the route of the stability of regular holonomic complexes.** `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` plans, as a theorem written here, the stability of the regular holonomic complexes of crystals under the six operations, for all maps of finite type of schemes of finite type over every field of characteristic 0; the maps need not be separated. The roadmap node `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` presupposes the same stability for the left modules on smooth separated schemes of pure dimension over ℂ, in its clauses (1), (3) and (5): under `f_+`, `f_!`, a general `f^†`, the external product and the restriction to the diagonal. The accepted roadmap MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory requests it from SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles as `gap:regular-holonomic-stability`; no roadmap states it as a target. In that case the two are one theorem ([HottaTakeuchiTanisaki] Theorem 6.1.5 for the direct and inverse images), and it is not proved twice. Until the provider is decided with `prompts/change.md`, `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` is under "Held", and the stability theorem of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` is not written; the definition of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` does not wait.

Candidate A, a roadmap node for the request. A node of its own in `plans/Roadmap/SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles/`, as a prerequisite and not a named target, in the form of `Infinity.derivedBimoduleTransfer` for `gap:derived-bimodule-sheaves`. It states the stability for the left modules on smooth separated complex schemes of pure dimension. `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` depends on it, and the theorem of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` takes its complex case from it and passes from a field of characteristic 0 to ℂ. This moves a case of a theorem that the decision of 2026-11-19 on D-modules lists under "Also written here", so it is a change of provider. Its obligations, none of which a node states today:

1. Descent to a small field. A bounded holonomic complex on a scheme of finite type over a field `k` of characteristic 0, with the maps of schemes in question, comes by extension of scalars from a subfield that is finitely generated over ℚ; such a field embeds in ℂ. In the plan only `DModules.boundedEquivariantExt_baseChange` of `plans-proof/` asks for a descent of this kind, for its own objects.
2. Regularity under extension of the field. For a general field, regularity is regularity after base change to an algebraic closure, and (4) of `DModules.regularHolonomicDModule` compares two algebraically closed fields. `DModules.scalarExtension` states that the extension of scalars preserves regular holonomic complexes; the reduction also needs that it reflects them.
3. The operations under extension of the field. `DModules.scalarExtension` gives the compatibility with `*`- and `!`-pullback and with the direct image along locally closed embeddings and proper maps. Missing, as three separate obligations:
   - the direct image along a separated map of finite type: from the two stated cases, by a factorization of the map into an open immersion and a proper map and the composition of direct images;
   - the direct image along a map of finite type that is not separated, for example from the line with a doubled origin to the point. Such a map has no such factorization. One route: a finite affine open cover of the source, whose finite intersections are separated, and the description of the direct image by the Čech nerve of the cover, from the descent of crystals (`DModules.rightCrystals_hDescent`);
   - duality, and with it the `!`-pushforward `f_! = 𝔻 f_* 𝔻` of `DModules.holonomicShriek`.
4. The dependencies of the stability theorem. `DModules.scalarExtension` depends on `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`, so a stability theorem that uses the extension of scalars cannot stay a clause of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`: it becomes a node of its own, above `DModules.scalarExtension`. `DModules.scalarExtension` also depends on `DModules.intermediateExtension`, and through it on `DModules.perverseHeart`, whose goodness speaks of the `*`- and `!`-extensions of a regular holonomic module from a stratum; these are regular holonomic by the stability. So the clause of `DModules.scalarExtension` on intermediate extensions must move above the stability theorem too, or the nodes form a cycle. The users of the stability (`DModules.perverseHeart`, `DModules.analyticDeRham`, `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, and the nodes that use these) then depend on the new node.
5. The external product. If the stability under the external product and the `!`-tensor product is taken from the complex case, the proof needs the comparison of the external product of right crystals with that of left D-modules. Today it is a proof obligation of `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, which depends on `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`; it must move to a node below the stability theorem.
6. From left modules on smooth separated schemes of pure dimension to crystals on schemes of finite type, and to all maps of finite type. For affine schemes: closed embeddings into affine spaces and the graph of a map, with the comparisons of the functors of crystals with the classical ones that `DModules.rightCrystals_equiv_dModules`, `DModules.deRhamPushforward` and `DModules.verdierDuality` state. For all schemes and maps of finite type, the maps that are not separated among them: gluing over finite affine open covers, because regular holonomic complexes are defined Zariski-locally and the direct image is computed from a cover as in obligation 3. This part is written here in every candidate.

Candidate B, the field comparison on the side of Tau Ceti. The roadmap node of candidate A asks for the stability over every field of characteristic 0, as a part beyond the request, with the rule that a declined part comes back. Its scope is that of the roadmap: the left modules on smooth separated schemes of finite type of pure dimension, whose maps are separated. SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles §9.7 has this shape for the stabilities that it states: "First establish the stated complex-field theorem, then prove compatibility under algebraically closed characteristic-zero extensions and descend geometric regularity to arbitrary characteristic-zero k." Then obligations 1 and 2 and the field comparison of obligation 3 are Tau Ceti's, in that scope, and obligation 4 does not arise, because the theorem written here does not use `DModules.scalarExtension`. Written here in candidate B: obligation 5, and obligation 6 in full, which is the passage to the crystals on schemes of finite type and to all maps of finite type, the maps that are not separated among them; it needs no extension of the field. Candidate B asks Tau Ceti for more than the request states.
