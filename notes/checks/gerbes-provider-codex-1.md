### Open objections

1. **Correct the band under change of test scheme.** `Stacks.EtaleGerbe` takes an arbitrary abelian sheaf \(A\) on the big étale site and identifies \(f^{-1}A_S\) with \(A_T\) for every \(f:T\to S\). In general there is only a comparison map. Take \(A=\mathbb G_a\), \(S=\operatorname{Spec}k\), and \(T=\operatorname{Spec}k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\) over an algebraically closed field. The small étale topoi are equivalent, but the comparison on global sections is \(k\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\), which misses \(\epsilon\). I would accept keeping the stated generality and changing band along \(f^{-1}A_S\to A_T\) after inverse image, with identity and composition coherence. For the invertible \(\mu_n\) used by the statement, the comparison is an isomorphism.
