## Mismatches

**M1. The sign convention is not fixed, so the node text does not determine the isomorphism of line bundles.** The plan node file, line 16 (`desc`). Verdict: *mismatch* (wording; the object meant is right).

- The construction hides two contractions of a line with its inverse: `det RΓ(X, ad(P)(−nD))`, and the constant line `det RΓ(X, ad(P⁰))` of the normalization of `Stacks.determinantBundle`. The first line has rank `dim G · (1 − g − n · deg D)`. Its parity changes with `n` when `dim G · deg D` is odd (for example `SL_2` and one point).
- In the Picard groupoid of ℤ-graded lines with the Koszul sign rule, each contraction is canonical: it pairs a line with its own inverse, so coherence fixes it. The result is then independent of `n`. [GLys] §5.2.1 (footnote to `e:rel det`, TeX line 4863) uses graded lines, and `Satake.criticalDeterminantGerbe` already speaks of the grading and of Koszul signs.
- With lines without grading, the naive contraction differs from the graded one by `(−1)^rank`. A writer who mixes the two readings (for example a graded relative determinant in `Satake.criticalDeterminantGerbe` and a naive contraction here) gets an identification that changes sign with `n`. So the sentence "the identification does not depend on n" is true only under a fixed convention. Neither this node nor `Sheaves.determinant` nor `Stacks.determinantBundle` names one.
- Effect: the four lines of the result have degree zero, and a constant sign changes the equivalence of gerbes only by the torsor of square roots of `−1`. So the Hecke functor and `IsLanglandsFunctor` do not change up to isomorphism. The unit test of the node and theorem (1) of the new proof node both fail under a wrong constant sign, so the convention must be fixed in this node, not only in the proof node (the planner's doubt 2 places it there).
- The divisor `D` depends on the lifts of the points; the node states independence of `n` only.

Replacement. Replace the two sentences from "The additivity of the determinant of perfect complexes" to "the identification does not depend on n and is natural in S." with:

> The determinant lines are ℤ-graded lines, graded by the rank, in the Picard groupoid of graded line bundles on S with the Koszul sign rule for the commutativity of the tensor product ([GLys] §5.2.1, footnote); the inverse of a line is its dual in this groupoid. The additivity of the determinant of perfect complexes (Sheaves.determinant) on the exact triangles RΓ(X, ad(P)(−nD)) → RΓ(X, ad(P)) → ad(P)/ad(P)(−nD) and RΓ(X, ad(P)(−nD)) → RΓ(X, ad(P′)) → ad(P′)/ad(P)(−nD), followed by the contraction of det RΓ(X, ad(P)(−nD)) with its inverse, identifies det RΓ(X, ad(P′)) ⊗ (det RΓ(X, ad(P)))^{-1} with the determinant of the second quotient tensored with the inverse of the determinant of the first. In h_out^* det ⊗ (h_in^* det)^{-1} the constant line det RΓ(X, ad(P⁰)) of Stacks.determinantBundle is contracted with its inverse in the same way. Each contraction pairs a line with its own inverse, so the coherence of the Picard groupoid determines it. Without the grading it is not determined: the rank of RΓ(X, ad(P)(−nD)) is dim G · (1 − g − n · deg D), and its parity changes with n when dim G · deg D is odd. Both sides of the identification have degree zero. This is the relative determinant on D_x of the lattice of P′ against the lattice of P, that is, the pullback of det_{Gr_G}. The identification is the same for every vector bundle contained in ad(P) and in ad(P′) whose two quotients are finite locally free over S; so it does not depend on n or on the lifts that define D, and it is natural in S.
