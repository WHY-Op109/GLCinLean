## 1. The problem

The plan's theory of D-modules is the theory of crystals on prestacks: `Dmod(Y) = IndCoh(Y_dR)` (`DModules.RightCrystals`). It is written here, and no roadmap has it. Eight nodes also plan classical facts about modules over the sheaf of differential operators of a smooth scheme: `DModules.Coherent`, `DModules.IsHolonomic`, `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`, `DModules.intermediateExtension`, `DModules.verdierDuality`, `DModules.holonomicShriek`, `DModules.deRhamPushforward`, and the ring `D_X` of `DModules.rightCrystals_equiv_dModules`. The accepted roadmap SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles (TauCetiRoadmap #1828) owns these facts for smooth schemes, and ComplexComparisonPartII (#848) owns the differential operators. `AGENTS.md`, "Sources of code", says: "Parts covered by a Tau Ceti roadmap go to Tau Ceti", because "a second copy here would compete with it". So the provider of each classical fact must be decided, and it is a change of provider, which is a big change.

Nodes of the change on the decomposition theorem (`proposal/decomposition-theorem`) are next to these: `DModules.analyticDeRham`, `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, `DModules.perverseHeart` and the `Constructible.*` nodes. This proposal does not change them, and it does not decide the analytification step that finding B4 lists.

### 2.7 The comparison between the roadmap's objects and the plan's

The plan needs more than the equivalence of categories. A theorem of the roadmap about `f^†`, `f_+` or `𝔻_Y` applies to the plan's `f^!`, `f_*` or Verdier duality only when the equivalence carries one functor to the other. Three comparisons are needed, each on homotopy categories:

1. `f^!` of right crystals is `f^†`, for a morphism of smooth schemes. [Crystals] Remark 5.5.6 states it for left crystals. The shift is right: `Υ_X` is the tensor product with `ω_X = Ω^n_X[n]`, so a right module `Ω^{n′}_Y ⊗ L` in degree 0 goes under `f^!` to the side change of `Lf^*L[n − n′]`, which is `f^†L` in the convention of line 29.
2. `f_*` of right crystals is the direct image of right modules. It holds by construction, because the node builds `f_*` from the transfer module.
3. Verdier duality is `𝔻_Y`.

These comparisons do not give the descent of the enhanced functors or their coherent composition; those stay with the nodes written here. One side of each is a functor of crystals, so they are written here. They are clauses of the nodes that define the crystal functors (`DModules.rightCrystals_equiv_dModules`, `DModules.deRhamPushforward`, `DModules.verdierDuality`) and not a new node: each is part of the interface of its functor, and a separate node would have the same dependencies. The comparison of the homotopy category of the enhanced derived category with `DerivedCategory` is generic and belongs to EnhancedDerivedSheaves E1.3 (`enhancedDerived.homotopyCategory`), in the roadmap node `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory`.

## 3. The options

**(a) Roadmap nodes for the classical facts; the enhanced constructions here.** Cost: eight roadmap nodes, edits of eight nodes, and the three comparison clauses of 2.7. Risk: the roadmap's theorems are weaker than the plan needs in three places (2.3), so these stay here and are proved on the roadmap's objects; Tau Ceti may write Layers 8–9 late, because they are a deep part of a roadmap whose headline is elsewhere; `Infinity.derivedBimoduleTransfer` is not a named target and may be declined. It follows "Sources of code" and "Lean code": nothing that Tau Ceti owns is written twice, each classical fact has the roadmap's natural generality, and each concept has one home.

**(b) The nodes stay here.** Cost: no plan change now; later the ring `D_X`, coherent and holonomic modules, the characteristic variety, Bernstein's inequality, Kashiwara's theorem and the intermediate extension are written here. Risk: a second copy of named targets of an accepted roadmap. It breaks "Sources of code" and "Never duplicate Mathlib or Tau Ceti". It is not the exception of an object "with which the goal cannot be reached even in principle": the plan's objects are equivalent to the roadmap's on smooth schemes.

**(c) A partial move: only the ring `D_X` and the definitions; the theorems stay here.** Cost: smaller roadmap nodes. Risk: the theorems that stay here (Noetherianity, Bernstein, finite length, the properties of the intermediate extension) are named targets of §§8.2, 9.3, 9.5 and 9.6, so this is (b) for them.

**(d) Reach the classical facts through a route that needs none of them**: define holonomic objects by a stratification with lisse restrictions, and duality only by the formula of [DG2] §5.3.1. Cost: a change of route for `DModules.IsHolonomic`; finite length, the properties of the intermediate extension and the regular objects still need the classical theory or new proofs. Risk: a parallel theory. Not chosen; the definition of Verdier duality by the formula is already the plan's.

**(e) Wait.** The audit notes that none of these nodes is ready soon. Cost: none now. Risk: the first step (`D_X` on a scheme) is ready in the plan, #43231 leaves it open, and without the roadmap node no worker is guided to it in the generality the plan needs.
