## Remarks

- **The provider split is sound in substance.** MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory §§1.2–1.3 own the complex Betti category and heart used by EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheaves §§5.9–5.10. The decomposition theorem stays with §5.10. `AnalyticStratified.toml:6` correctly marks the fixed-stratification category, goodness and its five consequences as prerequisite work beyond the named targets. The restricted category required by Springer §11.3 is concrete evidence for that prerequisite. The wider coefficient and operation clauses of the first two nodes serve existing users, including orbit goodness and Bott–Samelson parity. The fallback in `STATUS.md:145` is appropriate if Tau Ceti declines those clauses.

- **The embedding condition is a reasonable limit for the construction.** Ordinary triangulated categories lose descent information. For example, the nonzero map \(\mathbb C\to\mathbb C[2]\) on \(\mathbb P^1\) restricts to zero on both standard affine opens. This rules out gluing the ordinary chart functors just from their overlap isomorphisms. It leaves open a construction using coherent descent before passing to homotopy categories, or descent on the perverse hearts followed by a realization theorem. The latter should use the full algebraically constructible category before restricting to a fixed stratification. The global-embedding construction is the smaller obligation for the current scheme-level users. [BBD, Corollary 2.1.23](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~bernstei/Publication_list/publication_texts/faisceaux_pervers.pdf) · [Beilinson, Main Theorem 1.3 and Remark (a)](https://websites.umich.edu/~bhattb/teaching/mat731f15/Beilinson.pdf)

- **The statement’s parity proof retains the needed comparison.** `Spherical.toml:28–29` applies it over \(\mathbb C\) to regular holonomic IC objects on projective Schubert closures and their smooth strata. Its passage through \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) still serves all algebraically closed characteristic-zero ground fields. I found no downstream use of de Rham on an irregular holonomic complex. For the stratified equivalence, state the source subcategory using lisse cohomology of \(i_T^*M\) throughout. The converse on each smooth stratum follows from Deligne’s equivalence for regular connections, t-exactness, and the shift by \(\dim T\).

- **The shift in §1.5 is correct.** For \(f:\mathbb A^1\to\mathrm{pt}\), the transfer normalization gives \(f^\dagger\mathbb C=\mathcal O_{\mathbb A^1}[1]\), and normalized de Rham gives \(\mathbb C_{\mathbb A^1}[2]=f^!\mathbb C\). This tests the dimension shift directly. Smooth schemes of mixed dimension are handled componentwise.
