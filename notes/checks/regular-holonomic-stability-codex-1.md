## Objections, in order of weight

1. **Step (a) leaves the singular-chart comparison unproved.** Kashiwara for a closed immersion between smooth schemes proves preservation and reflection for that immersion. It does not by itself compare two embeddings of a singular affine \(Y\), which steps (b), (c), and (g) need. The same comparison is needed for every smooth ambient embedding allowed by `AnalyticDeRham.toml:6` and `RiemannHilbert.toml:6`. There is a route with the proposed inputs. Given two embeddings \(i_1,i_2\), form the closed embedding \(g=(i_1,i_2)\) and let \(p_r\) be the two projections. By crystal Kashiwara and composition, \(g_*M \simeq g_*g^!p_r^!i_{r*}M\). Apply the localization fibre from step (b) to prove regularity of \(g_*M\) from either embedding. Then apply the other projection’s direct image to recover regularity in the other embedding. Establish this before using embedding independence, and check agreement with the smooth definition on affine open covers by open base change and Zariski gluing. This route uses the new smooth theorem and avoids a separate proof through simple modules and intermediate extension. Evidence is `RegularHolonomic.toml:6`, steps (a)–(c), and [Crystals] §§2.5.4–2.5.6 and Proposition 4.2.5.
