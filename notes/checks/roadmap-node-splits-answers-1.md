## 2. The five decisions

1. **The id is `GLCinLean.Derived.commutativeAlgebraToConnectiveCommAlg`** (the checker's). It spells the content: the functor from `Derived.commutativeAlgebra` to the connective commutative algebra objects, which exists for every commutative ring, with no promise of an equivalence. `animatedToCAlg` is the roadmap's name of the target. The plan names no roadmap node so: it has `Derived.commutativeAlgebra` for `animatedAlgebras`, `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory` for `enhancedDerived`, `Infinity.dualizableCategory` for `IsDualizablePresentable`. “Animated” would also be a second term for the category that the plan calls `Derived.commutativeAlgebra` (“Writing”: one term for each concept). Every use is updated: two module plans of EDS, `Construction.toml`, `docs/blueprint-providers.md` and sections 5 and 6 of the proposal.
2. **`GLCinLean.lean` has the entry now** (Codex's side), as “Plan” in `AGENTS.md` asks: the record names the declaration “once that exists”. It stands under “Differences from the sources”, in a new subsection, because the subsection on [GLC1] is for the declarations of `GLCinLean.Statement` and the difference is from [AGKRRV] and [Crystals]. The citations are verified: [AGKRRV] §0.9.6 (`text.tex`, lines 2205–2206); [Crystals] §0.6.4 (“the ∞-category opposite to that of connective commutative DG algebras over k”, line 811); Higher Algebra, Theorem 7.1.2.13 (`Mod_R ≃ D(A)` symmetric monoidally), Proposition 7.1.4.11 (commutative DG algebras over a ℚ-algebra), Proposition 7.1.4.20 (simplicial commutative algebras and connective E∞-algebras over a ℚ-algebra) and Warning 7.1.4.21 (the simplicial commutative algebras are `PΣ(Poly_R)`). The checker's sentence is in the decision of section 5: “The first declaration written here over the animated algebras (`Derived.Prestack` or `Derived.ClassicalReduction`) names the reformulation in its doc comment.”
3. **The three dependencies are removed** (Codex's side). The rule is that a dependency stays only if the statement or the definition needs it.
   - `DModules.commutativeDAlgebra`: its statement is about commutative algebra objects of the left D_X-modules. It uses no animated k-algebra, and the comparison node compares over the ground ring only, so it gives no DG model in D-modules. The notion of a commutative algebra object comes with `Infinity.moduleCategory`, which the node lists. After the removal the node lost no transitive dependency: through `DModules.RightCrystals` it still has both commutative-algebra nodes, `Infinity.monoidalCategory`, `Infinity.derivedVect` and the t-structure node.
   - `DModules.compactSupportedHomology_kunneth`: its statement is an equivalence `H(Y×Z) ≃ H(Y) ⊗ H(Z)` in `Infinity.derivedVect`. The Künneth theorem it names is about the homology of complexes (`HomologicalComplex.isIso_homologyKunneth_of_isSplitMono_of_isSplitEpi`). Neither uses a t-structure of a stable ∞-category.
   - `Satake.cohomologyFiberFunctor`: the cohomology objects of an object of `Vect` are those of `DerivedCategory`, through the equivalence of homotopy categories that `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory` gives. No truncation functor or heart of `Infinity.tStructure` is used.
   - Both nodes keep `Infinity.derivedVect` and still have the t-structure node transitively.
4. **The sentence “This is the order of the roadmaps”** is replaced by Codex's five sentences (“E1.3 uses the enhancement … as separate pull requests.”), followed by the checker's fact: “§6.0 needs EnhancedDerivedSheaves E0.4 and §0.1; the new node holds the check of §6.0 that its t-structure on enhanced derived modules is the standard one of §0.1.” Codex's text does not name E0.4 and does not say what the new node is.
5. **The t-structure node stays in `plans/Roadmap/EnhancedDerivedSheaves/`.** The object is the standard t-structure of §0.1 (README line 431). HermitianKTheoryOfPoincareCategories names EnhancedDerivedSheaves as its supplier (README line 15: “its §0.1 supplies the standard t-structure on enhanced derived modules”) and lists it under the needs of §6.0 (line 922); a node of that area would make the consumer the provider. By §6.0 (line 913) a t-structure of a stable ∞-category is a `CategoryTheory.Triangulated.TStructure` on its homotopy category, so the transported structure is one without more work; only the agreement of the lifted truncation functors is the check of §6.0 (line 920). The node and `docs/blueprint-providers.md` say this. The counts stay 28 and 1. The worker: an author worker on EnhancedDerivedSheaves gets the module `Roadmap.EnhancedDerivedSheaves.EnhancedDerivedTStructure` when `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory` and `Infinity.tStructure` are at the pin (section 5, “Text for Workers”). No such worker runs now.
