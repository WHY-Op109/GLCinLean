## 4. Options

A constraint first: a roadmap node is written in Tau Ceti, which cannot import this project. So a roadmap node depends only on roadmap nodes (`plans/README.md`), and a node that a roadmap node needs is itself a roadmap node.

1. **Keep the nine here.** This writes in this project what an accepted roadmap owns, against "Sources of code".
2. **Move all nine as they are.** `GLCinLean.AffineGrassmannian` depends on `LoopGroups.LoopGroup` and `Derived.IsIndscheme`, which are written here, and it carries the action of `Aut(O)` and the prestack form, which no roadmap states; `Satake.convolution` is a functor on D-modules, which no roadmap states. So these two cannot move whole.
3. **Move the part that the roadmap states, keep the rest here, as the decision on the loop group did** (`LoopGroups.FormalLoopGroup` in Tau Ceti, `LoopGroups.LoopGroup` here). The node that keeps plan-specific content keeps its id here, and the roadmap's object gets a new roadmap node below it. A node whose whole content is geometry over Tau Ceti's objects moves with its id.

Within option 3, two points have their own alternatives.

- `Groups.iwahoriWeylLength`. (a) A roadmap node of SheafTheoreticSmithTheory, as a prerequisite of §3.0 that is not a named target. (b) A roadmap node of ReductiveGroupsPartII (§RG2.4.1) or of LieHighestWeight (Layer 10). (c) Keep it here. (c) is not possible, because `AffineGrassmannian.iwahoriStratification` needs it in Tau Ceti. (b) names a roadmap whose target is another object: §RG2.4.1 is for `N(K)/Z(K)₀` and rests on the parahoric subgroups of RG2.3, and Layer 10 is for a root system without a central torus and has no length. Two more roadmaps state the Coxeter structure of an affine Weyl group on other carriers: KacMoody Layer 2, §2.1 (the Weyl group of a realization of a generalized Cartan matrix; at the pin as `TauCeti.CartanRealization.weylCoxeterSystem`) and ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.1 *affine-weyl-group* (`AffineWeylGroup.coxeterSystem`, for a valued root datum). The node and its first item build on the first and prove the Coxeter property once.
- Clauses that the plan needs and the roadmap does not state, but that follow from its statements (the `I`-orbits on `Gr`, the map of the Demazure varieties to `Gr`, the converse and the purity of [MV] Theorem 3.2). (a) Ask for them in the same roadmap node, with the rule of `LoopGroups.coordinateChange`: if Tau Ceti's review declines a clause, it comes back through `prompts/change.md`. (b) Split each into a node written here. (b) proves here, on Tau Ceti's objects, clauses that have the same proof as their neighbours.
