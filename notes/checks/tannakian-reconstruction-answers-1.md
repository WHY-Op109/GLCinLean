## My own second reading

I read each node that the branch adds or rewrites once more for the codomain of each functor and its supplier, the inputs of each proof step, the quantifiers and universes, and the degenerate cases. Found and fixed:

1. `Tannaka.finiteType_iff_exists_tensorGenerator`: the definition of a tensor generator did not say that the exponents `a` and `b` may differ from one summand to the next. With one pair `(a, b)` for all summands the character `t` of `𝔾_m` would not be a tensor generator, against the node's own check.
2. The same node described the pinned theorem on faithful comodules loosely (“of the comodule and of its dual”, “over a field”). The statement is for a comodule with a finite basis over a commutative ring, with the matrix coefficients and their images under the antipode.
3. `Tannaka.neutralReconstruction`, clause (5): the competing isomorphism is “in the form of (3)”, and `H` is in the universe of `K`; the check for `FGComoduleCat K H` names the forgetful functor of the prerequisite.
4. `Satake.cohomologyFiberFunctor`: the weight functors need the Borel subgroup, not only the torus; the heading now says “the Borel subgroup and the maximal torus T of its pinning”. The group algebra is that of `X_*(T)` written multiplicatively, which is what `MonoidAlgebra` asks.
5. `Satake.sphericalHeartDual`: two inputs of the proof were not in `deps`: the equivariant category in which `inv` acts (`DModules.EquivariantIndscheme`) and the functors of the base change (`DModules.holonomicShriek`). Added. The summary of Proposition 1.20 now says “under its conditions (a) to (d)”.
6. `Satake.tannakaRootDatum_equiv_dual`: “its hull” could be read as the hull of `IC_λ`; it is the hull of the object. The hypotheses of the reductivity theorem (characteristic 0, finite type, geometric connectedness) are named.
7. `Satake.tannakaGroup`: for a torus the Hopf algebra “is identified with” the group algebra (by clause (5)), not “is”.
8. The proposal said that `plans-proof/` is not changed; it now lists the two places that are.
9. Degenerate cases read again and found right: the zero category (excluded by `End(𝟙) = K`); the trivial group in all seven nodes (`B = K`, `r = 0`, the zero object as tensor generator, no object in the connectedness criterion, the counit as the map of `Tannaka.reconstructionMap`); the zero object in the two hulls (excluded as the sum of no copies of the unit; a tensor generator only through the exponent 0); a nontrivial finite group (finite type, not connected, by its regular representation); characteristic 2 (the super vector spaces are then no counterexample; `μ_2` is still a closed subgroup of `𝔾_m` with a restriction functor that is not full); characteristic `p` (`α_p` for the converse of the connectedness criterion; the finite-type and closed-immersion criteria have no restriction).
