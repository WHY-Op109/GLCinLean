## 1. The reduction to ℂ promises more than the plan supplies

**Fixed in `c19d157`: the nodes no longer choose a route. The reduction is a candidate for the separate change, stated below with its obligations.**

Codex is right on both points.

- `DModules.scalarExtension` states that the functor "commutes with *- and !-pullback and with pushforward along locally closed embeddings and proper maps, hence with intermediate extension and external products". It states nothing for the direct image along a general map, for the !-pushforward along a general map, or for duality. The sentence of `riemann-hilbert-provider-answers-2.md` that the compatibility of the six operations with the extension of the field "is in `DModules.scalarExtension`" is wrong for these three.
- The comparison of the two external products is a proof obligation of `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, which depends on `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`. A stability proof that uses it would need it below that node.

What changed. `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` and `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` keep the condition on the order of work. In place of "whose theorem then reduces a field of characteristic 0 to the complex numbers" they say: "That change chooses the provider of that case and the route of the theorem written here; this node chooses neither." The roadmap node and `docs/blueprint-providers.md` still name the roadmap node for the request as "one candidate" and say that it is a change of provider; they state no route.

### The open item for the separate change

`riemann-hilbert-provider-answers-4.md` has the corrected text of this item, for "Open items" of `STATUS.md`: obligations 3 and 6 and candidate B below claim more than holds for maps that are not separated. The text below is the text that round 4 judged. The branch does not edit `STATUS.md`.

**The provider and the route of the stability of regular holonomic complexes.** `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` plans, as a theorem written here, the stability of the regular holonomic complexes of crystals under the six operations, on schemes of finite type over every field of characteristic 0. The roadmap node `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` presupposes the same stability for the left modules on smooth separated schemes of pure dimension over ℂ, in its clauses (1), (3) and (5): under `f_+`, `f_!`, a general `f^†`, the external product and the restriction to the diagonal. The accepted roadmap MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory requests it from SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles as `gap:regular-holonomic-stability`; no roadmap states it as a target. In that case the two are one theorem ([HottaTakeuchiTanisaki] Theorem 6.1.5 for the direct and inverse images), and it is not proved twice. Until the provider is decided with `prompts/change.md`, `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` is under "Held", and the stability theorem of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` is not written; the definition of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` does not wait.

Candidate A, a roadmap node for the request. A node of its own in `plans/Roadmap/SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles/`, as a prerequisite and not a named target, in the form of `Infinity.derivedBimoduleTransfer` for `gap:derived-bimodule-sheaves`. It states the stability for the left modules on smooth complex schemes. `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` depends on it, and the theorem of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic` takes its complex case from it and passes from a field of characteristic 0 to ℂ. This moves a case of a theorem that the decision of 2026-11-19 on D-modules lists under "Also written here", so it is a change of provider. Its obligations, none of which a node states today:

1. Descent to a small field. A bounded holonomic complex on a scheme of finite type over a field `k` of characteristic 0, with the maps of schemes in question, comes by extension of scalars from a subfield that is finitely generated over ℚ; such a field embeds in ℂ. In the plan only `DModules.boundedEquivariantExt_baseChange` of `plans-proof/` asks for a descent of this kind, for its own objects.
2. Regularity under extension of the field. For a general field, regularity is regularity after base change to an algebraic closure, and (4) of `DModules.regularHolonomicDModule` compares two algebraically closed fields. `DModules.scalarExtension` states that the extension of scalars preserves regular holonomic complexes; the reduction also needs that it reflects them.
3. The operations under extension of the field. `DModules.scalarExtension` gives the compatibility with `*`- and `!`-pullback and with the direct image along locally closed embeddings and proper maps. Missing: the direct image along a general map (from the two cases that are stated, by a compactification of the map and the composition of direct images), duality, and with it the `!`-pushforward `f_! = 𝔻 f_* 𝔻` of `DModules.holonomicShriek` along a general map.
4. The dependencies of the stability theorem. `DModules.scalarExtension` depends on `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`, so a stability theorem that uses the extension of scalars cannot stay a clause of `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`: it becomes a node of its own, above `DModules.scalarExtension`. `DModules.scalarExtension` also depends on `DModules.intermediateExtension`, and through it on `DModules.perverseHeart`, whose goodness speaks of the `*`- and `!`-extensions of a regular holonomic module from a stratum; these are regular holonomic by the stability. So the clause of `DModules.scalarExtension` on intermediate extensions must move above the stability theorem too, or the nodes form a cycle. The users of the stability (`DModules.perverseHeart`, `DModules.analyticDeRham`, `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, and the nodes that use these) then depend on the new node.
5. The external product. If the stability under the external product and the `!`-tensor product is taken from the complex case, the proof needs the comparison of the external product of right crystals with that of left D-modules. Today it is a proof obligation of `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`, which depends on `DModules.IsRegularHolonomic`; it must move to a node below the stability theorem.
6. From smooth schemes to schemes of finite type, and from left modules to crystals: by closed embeddings into affine spaces and the graph of a map, with the comparisons of the functors of crystals with the classical ones that `DModules.rightCrystals_equiv_dModules`, `DModules.deRhamPushforward` and `DModules.verdierDuality` state. This part is written here in every candidate.

Candidate B, the field comparison on the side of Tau Ceti. The roadmap node of candidate A asks for the stability over every field of characteristic 0, as a part beyond the request, with the rule that a declined part comes back. SpringerResolutionAndCharacteristicCycles §9.7 has this shape for the stabilities that it states: "First establish the stated complex-field theorem, then prove compatibility under algebraically closed characteristic-zero extensions and descend geometric regularity to arbitrary characteristic-zero k." Then obligations 1 to 3 are Tau Ceti's, obligation 4 does not arise (the theorem written here does not use `DModules.scalarExtension`), and 5 and 6 remain. It asks Tau Ceti for more than the request states.

Candidate C, the theorem stays here in full. It is consistent with the decision of 2026-11-19 as written. Against it: the correspondence of §1.5 cannot use a theorem of this project, so Tau Ceti proves the complex case for its own route, and `AGENTS.md`, "Lean code", forbids a second proof here of what Tau Ceti has.

The change that decides this compares the three candidates, and with A or B it revises the decision of 2026-11-19 for this theorem.

## 2. The provider notes overstate the removal of the direct C5.6 input

**Fixed in `c19d157`.**

`STATUS.md`, "Open items", "The inputs of the Schubert example of `DModules.perverseHeart`", says that the step "over C because the strata are simply connected" of that node "uses the Riemann–Hilbert correspondence for regular connections on a smooth variety (ComplexComparisonPartII C5.6 `deligneRiemannHilbert`), which the node does not name and does not depend on". So the sentence of `docs/blueprint-providers.md` "no node written here uses C5.6 directly" was false. It now reads: "`DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence` does not use C5.6 directly, and the tracker does not check C5.6 itself. The Schubert example of `DModules.perverseHeart` uses the correspondence of C5.6 for regular connections on a smooth variety; that node does not name the input and does not depend on it." The open item of `STATUS.md` stays. The branch does not change `DModules.perverseHeart` beyond the words of round 0 on its scope.

For the same reason one more sentence of that paragraph is narrower now: "No node written here analytifies" is "No node written here constructs the analytification of a scheme or of a D-module"; the note `riemann-hilbert-provider.md` has the same correction, in the commit that adds this file.

A remark for the open item on the Schubert example: clause (6) of `DModules.regularHolonomicDeRham` is the statement that the example needs over ℂ (a regular holonomic module whose de Rham complex is a shifted local system is a vector bundle with a regular integrable connection, and regular connections correspond to local systems). When the input of the example is named, it can be that clause in place of C5.6; the dependency then puts `DModules.perverseHeart` above the roadmap node, which is under "Held" until the provider of the stability is decided.
