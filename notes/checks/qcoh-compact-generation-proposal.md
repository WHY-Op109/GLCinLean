## 1. The problem

`Sheaves.passable` (module `GLCinLean.AlgebraicGeometry.Sheaves.Passable`, provider "here") defined a passable prestack and asked for its example in the same node: "every quasi-compact separated scheme is passable (affine diagonal, compact structure sheaf, and QCoh compactly generated, hence dualizable); this is proved with the definition". The deep part of the example is that QCoh of a scheme is compactly generated by perfect complexes (Thomason–Trobaugh, Neeman, Bondal–Van den Bergh). An accepted Tau Ceti roadmap states this as a target, in triangulated form. So the node planned to write here what a roadmap owns ("Sources of code" in `AGENTS.md`).

The nodes that the problem touches, at `ebbb29c`: `Sheaves.passable`, `Sheaves.passable_tensorBaseChange`, `Sheaves.qcSeparatedScheme_oneAffine` (it uses the example), `Sheaves.schemeQCoh`, `Infinity.compactObject`, `Infinity.compactGeneration`, `Infinity.dualizableCategory`, `Infinity.homotopyCategoryPretriangulated`, and the held `Sheaves.QCoh`. ### 2.1 What SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.18 states

- **The category** (README line 5041; `Suggested.lean` lines 549–565). `DQCoh X` is "the full triangulated subcategory of Mathlib's `DerivedCategory X.Modules` of complexes all of whose cohomology sheaves are quasi-coherent". `X.Modules` is the category of sheaves of modules on the Zariski site of `X`, and quasi-coherent is `SheafOfModules.IsQuasicoherent`. `Suggested.lean` defines `Coherent.DQCoh (X : Scheme.{u})` for every scheme, with no hypothesis.
- **Universes.** For `X : Scheme.{u}`, `X.Modules` has objects in `Type (u+1)` and morphisms in `Type u`, and Mathlib proves that it is a Grothendieck abelian category for `u`. `Suggested.lean` takes `HasDerivedCategory.standard`, whose morphisms are in `Type (u+1)`, and gives `DQCoh X` a `Category.{u+1}` instance. The coproducts of Stacks Lemma 36.3.1 are those indexed by sets of the universe `u`.
- **The target** (lines 5059–5061): "Prove `perfect_generator`: for `X` quasi-compact quasi-separated, `DQCoh X` is generated by a single perfect complex and is compactly generated by perfect complexes (Stacks, §36.15 (09IP), Lemmas 36.15.1–36.15.2, Theorem 36.15.3 (09IS))". `Suggested.lean` has no statement of it.
- **"Generated by a single perfect complex"** is Theorem 36.15.3: there is a perfect `P` such that an object `E` of `D_QCoh(O_X)` is zero if and only if `Hom(P[n], E) = 0` for all `n ∈ ℤ`.
- **"Compactly generated by perfect complexes"** adds that the generators are compact objects of `DQCoh X`. The plan reads it as: every perfect object is compact. The weaker reading is: the generator is compact. The plan needs the first reading, for every perfect object (`Sheaves.schemeQCoh_compactGeneration` (2)). If Tau Ceti has only the weaker statement, the first follows from `perfect_dual` and `totalDirectImage_coproduct` of §2.18: `Hom(K, E)` is the degree 0 cohomology of the derived global sections of the dual of `K` tensor `E`. The target does not state that every compact object is perfect (Stacks Proposition 36.17.1, tag 09M1).
- **Perfect complexes.** §2.18 uses the word (lines 5052–5060) and defines none. The predicate is `IsPerfectComplex` of SchemeKTheoryOperations §1.3 (README line 207; `Suggested.lean` line 63): a complex of module sheaves with, near every point, a quasi-isomorphism from a strictly perfect complex. It is not written.
- **The proof of Theorem 36.15.3** uses Lemma 36.15.1, which §2.18 cites and which Stacks states as a special case of Lemma 36.13.11. Lemmas 36.13.6 to 36.13.11 are targets of SchemeKTheoryOperations §3.2 (README lines 864–878). The target on Lemma 36.13.11 lists "SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.18, perfect generators" under its needs (line 878); the targets on Lemmas 36.13.6 to 36.13.10 do not (lines 870 and 874). The target on the supported generator (line 882) lists the generator too. For the case that Lemma 36.15.1 needs (no map to an object `E`, no support condition), the Stacks proof of Lemma 36.13.11 is an induction on the number of affine opens that uses Lemma 36.13.10, of which Lemma 36.13.8 (tag 08EI) is the case without a support condition, and the gluing of objects on two opens (Stacks Lemma 20.45.1, tag 08DG). Stacks proves Lemma 36.13.8 from Lemmas 36.13.6 and 36.13.7. Theorem 36.15.3 also uses Lemma 36.15.2, which §2.18 cites. So `perfect_generator` waits for Lemma 36.13.8 and not for the targets on Lemma 36.13.11 and on the supported generator, and there is no circle.
- **Hypotheses.** `X` quasi-compact and quasi-separated. No base scheme, no Noetherian hypothesis, no separatedness.
- **Users of the target in other accepted roadmaps.** ComplexComparisonPartII (lines 324, 2338, 2364) and SchemeKTheoryOperations (line 93: "§2.18 supplies … the perfect generator, including compact generation by perfect complexes").

### 2.4 The passage from `DQCoh X` to the enhanced category

Three facts are needed.

1. **The homotopy-category comparison**: the homotopy category of `Sheaves.schemeQCoh X` is `DQCoh X`, as a triangulated category, with the same small coproducts. The node `Sheaves.schemeQCoh` states it. In the roadmap the comparison of the categories is not a named target. It holds by the definition of E1.8, by `enhancedDerived.homotopyCategory` and `enhancedDerived.isStable` of E1.3 (line 553), and by the fact that the homotopy category of a full subcategory of a quasicategory is the full subcategory of the homotopy category. It is a prerequisite of the accepted target `quasicoherentDerived_affine`: E1.8 says "on homotopy categories this is §2.18's `DQCoh.affineEquiv`". That the perfect objects correspond in both directions under the comparison is the roadmap node `Sheaves.perfectDerived_iff_isPerfectComplex`, an item of the interface of SchemeKTheoryOperations §1.3 (line 215: "For qcqs X, membership is equivalent to local quasi-isomorphism to the strict sheaf models of §1.1.").
2. **The test on the homotopy category**: in a stable quasicategory with small coproducts, the mapping-space functor of an object preserves small filtered colimits if and only if `Hom` out of it in the homotopy category commutes with small coproducts (Higher Algebra, Proposition 1.4.4.1(3)). For a locally small category the first condition is compactness (E0.8, line 390: "C in `u + 1` and locally u-small"). The enhanced category of a scheme is locally small, as a full subcategory of `D∞(O_X)` (E1.3); `Sheaves.schemeQCoh` supplies this, and the corollary uses it before it applies the test. It is not a named target. It is a prerequisite of accepted targets: `rightAdjoint_preservesCoproducts_iff` (E3.3, line 1217) cites Proposition 1.4.4.1(3) "for compactness tested against coproducts in a stable category", the proof route of `IsLocallyWeaklyContractible.compactlyGenerated` (E2.5, line 2289) uses "the finite-support criterion of HA Proposition 1.4.4.1(3)". The node `Infinity.compactObject_iff_homotopyCategory` states it. Codex and the checker agree that it stays upstream.
3. **Presentability**: if moreover the homotopy category has small Hom sets, a small set of compact objects whose shifts detect zero makes the category presentable (Higher Algebra, Corollary 1.4.4.2). E3.3 and E2.5 do not cite this corollary, and no accepted target that needs it in this generality was found. So it is a node written here, in its general form: `Infinity.isPresentable_of_compactGenerators`.

With these three facts, compact generation of `Sheaves.schemeQCoh X` is a corollary of `perfect_generator` with no further geometry.

### 2.6 What each user needs

- **The three conditions of `Sheaves.passable`** ([OneAffineness] Definition 5.1.2; [GRQCoh] §3.5.1, with §3.3.1 for quasi-affine): the diagonal is schematic, quasi-compact and quasi-affine; `O_Y` is compact; `QCoh(Y)` is dualizable. The definition needs no theorem on schemes. Its test examples are the derived affine schemes.
- **The example on schemes.** The diagonal of every scheme is an immersion, and it is quasi-compact exactly when the scheme is quasi-separated; a quasi-compact immersion is quasi-affine (Stacks Lemma 29.13.6, tag 02JR). So the diagonal condition needs neither separatedness nor an affine diagonal. The other two conditions hold for quasi-compact quasi-separated schemes by `perfect_generator`. So "quasi-compact separated" is not the natural generality; "quasi-compact quasi-separated" is. [OneAffineness] §5.3.1 says "any quasi-compact quasi-separated algebraic space 𝒴 is passable as a prestack". [GRQCoh] §3.7.4 states the example through perfect stacks, which have affine diagonal by definition.
- **The comparison on derived test schemes.** The example and the ULA example are about QCoh of the prestack of a scheme, which is the scheme as a derived scheme. They need the equivalence of that QCoh with `Sheaves.schemeQCoh X`, with the unit and the perfect objects. No node states it, and no node gives the prestack of a classical scheme. The held `Sheaves.QCoh` names `Sheaves.schemeQCoh` among its `deps` and does not state the equivalence. #2450 §5.3 states an equivalence for the functor of points of `X` on ordinary rings. A route here: [GRQCoh] Theorem 1.3.4 (Zariski descent of QCoh of prestacks), compared with Zariski descent for `Sheaves.schemeQCoh` on the basis of affine opens, with affine equivalences that are natural for restriction. [GRQCoh] Proposition 1.4.4 (the limit over the affine opens) is for the schemes of that book, which are separated (its Chapter I.2, Remark 3.1.3: "Our definition gives what is usually called a separated scheme").
- **`Sheaves.passable_tensorBaseChange`** needs the definition only.
- **`Sheaves.qcSeparatedScheme_oneAffine`** needs passability of its scheme: the example. Its proof uses the affine diagonal, so its scope stays "separated".
- **Two more users of the underlying fact**: `Sheaves.properPerfectPushforward` uses that `QCoh(X)` is dualizable for the curve `X`, and the example "Dmod(S) is ULA over S" of `DModules.IsULAModuleCategory` ([RaskinCPSI] Example B.6.2) uses that `QCoh(S)` is compactly generated by perfect objects.
