- The node says "a sheaf A of abelian groups on the étale site of affine schemes over the base field; A restricts to the étale site of every test scheme", and lists `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.smallEtaleTopology` under "Upstream declarations used".
- [GLys] §1.3.2 (`text.tex` line 1497) uses the big site: "the étale … sheafification of `B^i(𝒜)` in the category `(Sch^aff)_{/𝒴}`".
- The two readings give the same 2-groupoid, but different work. On the site of affine schemes over S, pullback along S′ → S is restriction to a slice, the band is literally the restriction of A, and the roadmap's base change `RootGerbe_n(L)×X T≃RootGerbe_n(L|T)` is the compatibility equivalence that `Stacks.rootGerbe` names. On the small étale site, pullback needs the inverse image with stackification, and for a general A (for example `𝔾_m`, or `μ_n` with n not invertible) the inverse image of the band is not the restriction of A, so clause (1) also needs a change of band. The roadmap's `rootGerbe` has fibres over every T → X, so it is an object of the big site; `Stacks.schemeRootGerbe` does not name the site or the topology either.
- Fix: state in `Stacks.EtaleGerbe`, `Stacks.schemeRootGerbe` and `Stacks.rootGerbe` one site (I recommend affine schemes over S with the étale topology, as [GLys]); name `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etaleTopology` (same Mathlib file) in place of `smallEtaleTopology` if that site is chosen.
