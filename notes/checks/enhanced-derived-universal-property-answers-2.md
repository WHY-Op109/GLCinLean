## Is the split still the right choice?

Yes. The other result, "no change of the node, only the corrected items and texts", is worse by "Plan" and "Lean code" of `AGENTS.md`, for three reasons. None of them is a saving: the count before `DGCatCarrier` is about 28 with or without the split, and only one node loses a wait.

- **The two results of G2.11 must be nodes in either case.** The route of `Infinity.derivedVect` needs the localization of a quasicategory and the localization of a symmetric monoidal category. They belong to GeneralizedCohomology, not to EnhancedDerivedSheaves. A roadmap result that the plan needs and that has no node is a recorded defect: `STATUS.md`, "Roadmap targets without a plan node", says that `lake exe tracker ready` does not see such waits. So `Infinity.IsLocalization` and `Infinity.symmetricMonoidalLocalization` are nodes of `plans/Roadmap/GeneralizedCohomology/` with or without the split.
- **With `Infinity.IsLocalization` as a node, a clause inside `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory` is wrong.** The node would depend on a notion of G2.11 that its definition (the dg nerve, its stability, the comparison with `DerivedCategory`) does not use, and every user would wait for it. The decision "Faster within Tau Ceti's rules" keeps a dependency between two roadmap nodes "only when the statement or the definition of the upper node needs the lower one". The theorem is the only part that uses the notion, so it is the node that depends on it. "Lean code" asks for one concept per file and for each definition's universal property: the split has the definition and, directly above it, its universal property as a theorem.
- **Each node is then the statement of its own target items.** "Plan" deletes a roadmap node only when its declaration is at the pin with its interface and meaning checked. Tau Ceti lands E1.3 in parts, and the universal property is the last and largest part. After the split, `Infinity.enhancedDerivedCategory` is folded in when `enhanced-derived-stable` and `enhanced-derived-category` are at the pin, and the nodes that use only the object then name the upstream declaration. Without the split it stays open until the theorem lands, though its object is complete.

The cost is three more roadmap nodes to keep current. It is small next to a plan whose dependencies say what each node uses.
