### 1. Shriek pushforward has the wrong unrestricted base-change law

**`DModules/HolonomicShriek.toml`, lines 6–7.** The node asks for composition and “!-base change” for every finite-type map. For a Cartesian square with vertical map \(f\) and change of base \(g\), the general formula for holonomic objects is \(g^*f_!\simeq f'_!g'^*\). The formula with \(g^!\) belongs to \(f_*\). The latter is precisely [DG2] §5.2.1(iv).

There is a counterexample among the proposal’s own checks. Take \(j:\mathbb G_m\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^1\), \(i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^1\), and the trivial connection on \(\mathbb G_m\). The fibre product is empty. But \(j_+\mathcal O/\mathcal O\simeq\delta_0\), so duality gives an inclusion \(\delta_0\hookrightarrow j_!\mathcal O\). By closed-immersion adjunction, \(i^!j_!\mathcal O\ne0\). Unrestricted !-pullback base change would make it zero. The same wording is used by the downstream `plans-proof/GLCinLean/AlgebraicGeometry/DModules/IndHolonomic.toml`, line 13.

**Change.** State the \(g^*\) formula, add `DModules.starPullback` to the dependencies, and state the \(g^!\) version for smooth \(g\) by the relative-dimension shift, or for other hypotheses that are actually proved. State the partial adjunction as
\[
\operatorname{Map}(f_!M,N)\simeq\operatorname{Map}(M,f^!N)
\]
for bounded holonomic \(M\) and **every ambient** \(N\). The Whittaker and ind-holonomic users need this quantifier. Correct the affected proof-plan line and test any required diagonal square under its own hypotheses. Under the project’s `wrong` rule, the unrestricted !/! assertion cannot remain a ready proof obligation. [DG2, §§5.2–5.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.5351)
