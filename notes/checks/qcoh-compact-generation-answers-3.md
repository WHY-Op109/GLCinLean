**B2. The false sentence on the universe. Applied with the report's wording.** I checked the argument: for a family with a coproduct E, the maps E → E that are the inclusion on a subset of the non-zero members and zero elsewhere are different for different subsets, because each inclusion is a split monomorphism; so the set of non-zero members is u-small when the Hom sets are. `PerfectGenerator.toml`, input (b): "On that model `IsCompactForCoproducts K` implies statement (1), because a type of `Type u` is small for `Type (u+1)`, and it is equivalent to statement (1), because the Hom sets are u-small: a family with a coproduct has only a u-small set of non-zero members. On a model with morphisms in `Type u` it is statement (1). Either form passes the interface check." Same claim elsewhere: the guidance, step (e), in a shorter form because of the limit of length; the proposal, section 7, doubt 2.

**B3. The supplier of the small Hom sets. Applied; one account with Codex 2.** `Sheaves.schemeQCoh` supplies it: "The mapping spaces of this category are small, as those of `D∞(O_X)`, of which it is a full subcategory (E1.3: `enhancedDerived.mappingSpaces`, `enhancedDerived_isPresentable`)". `Sheaves.schemeQCoh_compactGeneration` uses it once, before the test: "C is locally small, and so the Hom sets of its homotopy category are small, because C is a full subcategory of `D∞(O_X)`, whose mapping spaces are small …; Sheaves.schemeQCoh supplies this". `DerivedQCoh.toml` gets "(by K-injective resolutions in a Grothendieck abelian category; this is not an item of §2.18)".

Suppliers, all reached by `deps`: `Derived.commutativeAlgebra`, `Derived.affineModules`, `Infinity.tStructure` (added), `Derived.IsArtinStack`. `Sheaves.properPerfectPushforward` left the `deps` and [GRQCoh] Proposition 2.2.2 the `source`.
