### F2. `Infinity.tStructure` belongs to HermitianKTheoryOfPoincareCategories §6.0, and its prerequisite gets a node (small, applied)

- **Evidence.** The node named “Coherent model diagrams and stable enhancements” as its provider. Neither the old F6.5 nor G2.11 contains the word “t-structure”. EnhancedDerivedSheaves §0.1 provides only “the standard cohomological t-structure and its `D⁺` and `Dᵇ` subcategories” of the enhanced derived category of a ringed site. HermitianKTheoryOfPoincareCategories (TauCetiRoadmap #1266, merged on 2026-11-17, before the last compared commit) states the notion in §6.0 “Truncations and the Postnikov t-structure over coherent rings”: “For a supplied stable ∞-category `C`, use Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Triangulated.TStructure` on hC. Lift its halves to full subcategories of `C` and construct the enhanced truncation functors with the truncation adjunctions” (Higher Algebra, Definition 1.2.1.4). This is the object of the node, which names the same Mathlib structure.
- **Applied.** The node is in `plans/Roadmap/HermitianKTheoryOfPoincareCategories/TStructure.toml` and quotes §6.0. Its claim did not change. Its text says that the definition is for every universe (§6.0 takes a bundle of a small quasicategory from GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §6.1a, and the users here are large categories) and that §6.0 uses homological indices. The roadmap nodes now belong to nine areas.
- **The prerequisite.** Mathlib's `Triangulated.TStructure` needs the homotopy category to be preadditive with a zero object, a shift by ℤ with additive shift functors, and pretriangulated. `Functor.TExact`, which four users of the node need, asks for a triangulated functor. No roadmap states these for a stable quasicategory as a target, and no node stated them. The new node `Infinity.homotopyCategoryPretriangulated` (`plans/Roadmap/EnhancedDerivedSheaves/StableHomotopyCategory.toml`) states the triangulated structure of the homotopy category ([LurieHA] Theorem 1.1.2.14) and the triangulated functor that an exact functor induces. `Infinity.tStructure` depends on it. It is a roadmap node, because a roadmap node depends on it.
- **Its area.** EnhancedDerivedSheaves, as a prerequisite and not a named target. Three targets of that roadmap cannot be stated without the structure: E0.6 `dgNerve_shift_cone_comparison` (“the triangulated structure that stability induces on the homotopy category (HA Theorem 1.1.2.14) is the pretriangulated structure Mathlib puts on the homotopy category of complexes”), and E5:abstract.4 `stableMonoidal_homotopyCategory` and `exactMonoidalFunctor_triangle`. Its E0.4 holds the suspension and the exact functors that the structure is built from, and E0.6 is the first target in the order of the roadmaps that needs it. HermitianKTheoryOfPoincareCategories §6.0 and GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §6.1a also use the structure; both list EnhancedDerivedSheaves E0.4 under their needs and build none of it. If the review of Tau Ceti declines the node as outside the roadmap, it comes back through `prompts/change.md`; the node says so.
- **How it was missed.** The last audit checked that each quoted passage title exists, and not that the passage states the object. A search of the accepted roadmaps for “t-structure” found §6.0. GitHub's code search does not index large files, so the search is not complete.

The session should read the first pull request of `author-infinity` for this point. The open #2270 (InfinityCategoryTheory, head unchanged) would give these targets a second owner.
