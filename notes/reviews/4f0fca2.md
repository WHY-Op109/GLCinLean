## For the owner: mismatches and unsure verdicts

- **Mismatch (item 1, `Spectral.localization`, deps and justification).** The formula `Loc = (id × p)_! ∘ ev^*` is the paper's functor. But the node says that the left adjoint `(id × p)_!` of QCoh pullback along `LS_H × Ran → LS_H` "exists because Ran(X) is pseudo-proper (Hecke.ranPseudoProper)". Pseudo-properness ([GaitsgoryAtiyahBott] Prop. 1.5.2, Cor. 1.5.4) gives the left adjoint of `f^!` on `Shv^!` (D-modules), with base change by schemes. It does not give a left adjoint of a QCoh pullback whose base `LS_H` is a derived stack. The missing input is a Künneth or base-change statement such as `QCoh(LS_H × Ran) ≃ QCoh(LS_H) ⊗ Dmod(Ran)` (the form [GLC2] §17.6.1 uses), which needs `QCoh(S × (X^I)_dR) ≃ QCoh(S) ⊗ Dmod(X^I)` for affine `S` and the dualizability of `Dmod(X^I)` (or of `QCoh(LS_H)`). No node that the deps reach supplies this. The nearest nodes (`Sheaves.passable_tensorBaseChange`, `LocalSystems.qcohRigid`, which the old version depended on) are now in `plans-proof/`.
- **Unsure (item 1, which Ran).** The node, and `Hecke.ranSpace`, do not say that Ran is the de Rham Ran (`Ran(X) → Ran(X)_dR` is an isomorphism, [GaitsgoryOutline] §4.1.1). The construction gives the paper's functor only in that case, because QCoh along Ran must then be D-modules. `Hecke.ranPseudoProper` speaks of "the Ran prestack used for right crystals" as something to be identified with the colimit of the `X^I`, which suggests that `Hecke.ranSpace` is the non-de-Rham one. If it is, `QCoh(LS_H × Ran)` is the wrong category.
- **Unsure (item 5, examples of the statement).** The cut moved `Examples.trivialGroupFunctor_equiv` and `Examples.torusCoarse_equiv` to `plans-proof/`, because they go through `Langlands.coarse`. They were the only examples that tested the normalizations of the exponential, the twist and the Poincaré object. After the cut, no example in `plans/` tests `Statement.Loc`, `Statement.heckeActionRight`, `Statement.vacuumPoincare` or `Statement.RepRan`. AGENTS.md asks that a placeholder be replaced "only together with examples that show the new definition behaves as expected", and `plans/README.md` line 3 says the plan holds "the examples that rule out a trivial reading of its placeholders".
