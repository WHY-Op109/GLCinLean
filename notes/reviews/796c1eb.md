## Answers to the questions on `796c1eb`

**1. Is the definition of an étale μ_n-gerbe on a prestack right, and at the generality of the sources?** Yes. [GLys] §1.3.6 (`text.tex` 1548–1558) defines an `A`-gerbe on a prestack `Y` as a map from `Y` to `B²_et(A)`, the étale sheafification of the two-fold classifying prestack (§1.3.2, `text.tex` 1495–1507). A map from an affine scheme `S` to `B²_et(μ_n)` is a gerbe banded by μ_n on the étale site of `S`, and a map from `Y` is a point of the limit over the test schemes. That is the node's "compatible family … of gerbes banded by μ_n on the étale site of S", with the usual description of a banded gerbe (a stack in groupoids, locally nonempty, locally connected, automorphism sheaves identified with the band). So the node is the paper's object in another presentation. The generality is that of [GLC1] §1.1.3 (`functor.tex` 1384–1389): any "space", μ_n for any `n ∈ ℕ`. [GLys] §1.3 allows more bands (any group-like 𝔼_2-object over `Y`, in most examples a constant torsion group); the node excludes them on purpose, and the D-module twist of [GLys] §1.7.3 (`text.tex` 2010–2024) needs only a torsion subgroup of `k^×`, whose finite subgroups are the μ_n. So the node is not narrower than [GLC1] and not wider than [GLys]. The hypothesis on `n` is discussed in point 5.

**2. Are the listed operations all that the six nodes need?** By name, yes; as structure, not yet (point 2). `DModules.twistedCrystals` uses trivializations, the torsor between two of them, étale-local triviality, equivalences of gerbes and the tensor product of gerbes and of trivializations. `DModules.extraordinaryPullback` uses pullback and equivalences. `DModules.twistedPseudoProperShriek` uses pullback of gerbes and of trivializations, and étale-local triviality. `Hecke.sphericalAction` uses pullback, tensor product, inverse and the constraints. `Satake.criticalDeterminantGerbe` uses the root gerbe of a factorization line bundle, with its factorization, equivariance and multiplicative structure; these come from the line bundle once `R_n` is a symmetric monoidal functor natural in the prestack (point 3). `Satake.criticalTwist_matchesBunDeterminant` uses the three root-gerbe formulas and the functoriality of `R_n` in isomorphisms (point 3).

**3. Are the three root-gerbe formulas true and coherent?** Yes. `R_n(f^*L) ≃ f^*R_n(L)` holds by definition, since both have the pairs `(M, M^{⊗n} ≅ L|_S)` as objects over a test scheme `S` ([GLys] §1.4.2). For `R_n(L) ⊗ R_n(M) ≃ R_n(L ⊗ M)`: the functor `(L′, M′) ↦ L′ ⊗ M′` from `R_n(L) × R_n(M)` to `R_n(L ⊗ M)` is compatible with the multiplication `μ_n × μ_n → μ_n` on automorphisms, so it factors through the tensor product by a band-preserving map, and a band-preserving map of gerbes is an equivalence. For `R_n(L)^{-1} ≃ R_n(L^{-1})`: `M ↦ M^{-1}` is an equivalence of stacks that acts on automorphisms by `ζ ↦ ζ^{-1}`, which is a band-preserving equivalence from the inverse gerbe. The three are coherent because together they say that `R_n` is a homomorphism: on classes it is the boundary map of the Kummer sequence, `[R_n(L)] = δ[L]`, which is additive (#853 plans this as `rootGerbeClass`). The sentence "an n-th root of L is a trivialization" is [GLys] §1.4.2 and Remark 1.4.3 (`text.tex` 1628–1634). "This is not the root stack of a line bundle with a section" is true: SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.21 is titled "Quotient stacks, line bundles with a section, root stacks", and #853 §4.3 says the same about its own `rootGerbe`.

**4. Is "every such gerbe is étale-locally trivial on each test scheme" true?** Yes. A gerbe is locally nonempty, so an affine test scheme `S` has an étale cover `U → S` with an object over `U`. An object `x` of a gerbe banded by μ_n gives the band-preserving equivalence `y ↦ Isom(x, y)` to the stack of μ_n-torsors, which is the trivial gerbe. In the paper's presentation it is the statement that the test schemes on which the gerbe splits form a basis of the étale topology ([GLys] §1.7.1–1.7.2, `text.tex` 1961 and 1998).

**5. Does every node that uses a gerbe reach `DModules.etaleGerbe`, and `DModules.rootGerbe` where it needs a root gerbe? Is there a circular route?** There is no cycle; `DModules.etaleGerbe` reaches 13 nodes, none of them about gerbes or twists. `lake exe tracker show GLCinLean.DModules.etaleGerbe` lists `Derived.prestack`, `Stacks.groupTorsor`, `Stacks.algebraicStack` below it and `extraordinaryPullback`, `sphericalAction`, `twistedCrystals`, `rootGerbe` above it. Reachability:
