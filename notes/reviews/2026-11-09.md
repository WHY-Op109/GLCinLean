Two consequences need to remain visible during implementation:

1. `RootDatumDuality` can be empty, so `Setting` and the universally quantified theorem
   can be vacuous. The curve itself cannot be empty: `ConnectedSpace` includes nonemptiness.

   prevent this: the relevant laws remain absent. Replacement requires the actual constructions,
   comparison theorems and nontrivial examples, not extra axioms or certificates assumed in `Setting`.

These are existing mathematical gaps now exposed by tags and the blueprint. They are not
fixed by the successful build or by an axiom audit: opaque chosen objects can have no
`sorryAx` while having no enforced intended meaning.

## Standing-data comparison

The geometric part of `Setting`, separately from `RootDatumDuality`, is **same object,
different presentation**:

- It quantifies over every field of universe `u`, with characteristic zero and algebraic
  closure, rather than specializing to the complex numbers. G1 and L0 justify the field scope.
- `X → Spec k` is smooth of relative dimension one, proper and connected. Over the field in
  question this is the intended smooth complete connected curve. The empty curve is excluded.
- `G` and its dual are coordinate Hopf algebra objects. At Tau Ceti pin
  `e0610cce4004d628cbd9d2aafdbbb5459fe72938`,
  `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Reductive/Basic.lean`:71–97, 169–170 enforces finite type,
  smoothness, geometric connectedness and absence of a nontrivial geometric unipotent radical.
  `AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/Reductive/Basic.lean`:133–143 supplies the restricted
  anti-equivalence with reductive affine group schemes. No new wrapper changes this meaning.
- `Pinning` is real provider data, not a freely assigned simple-root index set:
  `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Pinning/Basic.lean`:88–103 includes a split maximal torus,
  a Borel containing it and simple-root-space trivializations. The zero-rank/torus case is
  not excluded. Existence for every reductive group is a remaining implementation obligation.
- The theta characteristic is a genuine invertible sheaf with a specified tensor-square
  isomorphism to relative differentials, rather than an equality or an assumed theorem.
  Provider evidence is `AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/Basic.lean`:119–120,
  `LineBundle/TensorProduct.lean`:65–79, and
  `Modules/Differentials/Basic.lean`:67–87. L3 asks for this choice.

## Hack-answer tests

Each of the 31 tagged declarations is covered by its H entry in the tables above.
The tests consider possible replacements/reinterpretations of the opaque data; they do
not claim Lean exposes or proves any particular replacement now.

- **H1 — nonempty carriers and named category choices.** Empty carriers are forbidden by
  `NonemptyType`, and object types cannot be empty. Singleton carriers and singleton object
  and functor types remain allowed. Choose every named geometric category to be that singleton
  model. Together with H2 and H4 this trivializes the theorem. Therefore nonemptiness is not
  a mathematical adequacy check.
- **H2 — predicates.** Compactness may be constantly false, making its implication vacuous;
  eventual coconnectivity may be constantly true. Either removes that test on objects. With
  singleton `LinIso`, the characterization can then hold for every functor. Actual filtered
  colimits and the union of integer truncation bounds must replace these predicates.
- **H3 — forgetful maps and operations.** Every map, selected regular module, identity,
  composite, evaluation, `Psi` and `LCoarse` can be the unique map/element on singleton
  carriers. No law relates their evaluations. This passes the current typing but does not
  construct an action, a t-structure or a functor.
- **H4 — `LinIso`.** Choosing every type singleton makes every functor pair naturally
  equivalent by fiat; inverses then exist using `Nonempty (LinFun D C)`. Choosing every type
  empty makes the characterization impossible for an inhabited setting, so the theorem's
  existence conjunct would fail. In particular, the code correctly supplies no automatic
  `Nonempty (LinIso F G)` instance.
- **H5 — formulas built over placeholders.** `LinFun.IsEquivalence` and
  `IsLanglandsFunctor` add the right quantified formulas, not extra categorical laws.
  The singleton/always-true choices above make both formulas true. These definitions
  remain tagged and must not be counted as completed geometric constructions.
- **H6 — duality and settings.** If `RootDatumDuality G Gd` is always empty, `Setting k`
  is empty and the main theorem is vacuous. If it is always singleton, every two groups
  qualify as dual; that also fails the intended meaning. The repair is a real integral
  root-datum construction and explicit nontrivial examples, not a new assumption that
  selected settings exist.
