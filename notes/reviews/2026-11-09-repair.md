### G1: the Satake construction is no longer hidden behind a name

The geometric inputs in the Satake, affine-Grassmannian, loop-group and D-module modules
match [MV] §§2–12. In particular, projective weight-functor representers and reconstruction
are real inputs: `math-0401222v5/src/Satake.tex:2972–3468`, §§9–11. The existing
`TauCeti.Tannaka.pointsFunctorIsoTensorAutFunctor` reconstructs a supplied Hopf algebra; it
does not supply the Hopf algebra of the spherical category. The new reconstruction node
correctly retains that missing construction.

The plan also retains the difference between the heart equivalence and a full derived
equivalence. [RaskinCPSI] §§6.30–6.33 (`RaskinCPSI-2018-09-05/cpsi.txt:3630–3735`) constructs
the regular-comodule/factorization enhancement; [GLys] §9.2.1 supplies the corresponding
naive functor. The determinant normalization is separate. MV's complex constructible proof,
and its étale variant, are not silently used as an arbitrary-field de Rham theorem.

`Groups.coweightParity` in `AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Parity.toml:4` uses the sum of positive
roots, a character, for the coweight pairing. The Whittaker `twoRho` is a cocharacter obtained
from positive coroots. These are no longer conflated. [GLys] §§5.2.2–5.2.3
(`1608.00284v7/src/text.tex:4886–4925`) supports the former. The direct Tau Ceti root-sum
and even-pairing leaves are used; the resulting central element has order dividing two,
so trivial parity for a torus is included.
