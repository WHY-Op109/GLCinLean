The transition functors C ↦ QCoh(S′) ⊗_{QCoh(S)} C, for S′ → S affine, preserve limits, because QCoh(S′) is a dualizable QCoh(S)-module. So limits of sheaves of categories are computed on each S. **Item 4: good stratifications.**
- *Definition.* The definition "good with coefficients E" (Rj_{T*}L ∈ D^b_S for every finite-rank local system L on every stratum) is right. "j_{T!}L is always in D^b_S" is right: its stalks are L on T and 0 elsewhere, and strata are disjoint.
- *Equivalence with duality.* The equivalence with Verdier-duality stability is right. Every object of D^b_S is a finite iterated extension of shifted j_{T!}L. Under the frontier condition a stratum not in the closure of another is open, so the open–closed triangles and the truncations on a stratum give this. And 𝔻(j_{T!}L) ≃ Rj_{T*}(L^∨)[2 dim T] on a smooth complex stratum.
- *Whitney umbrella.* The counterexample is right. The normalization (u, v) ↦ (uv, v, u²) is a homeomorphism over the complement, with two points over each nonzero point of the axis and one over 0. So H⁰(Rj_*E) has ranks 2 and 1 along the axis. IC = ν_*E[2] has the same rank change, and i^!E fails through the localization triangle. The strata are smooth and connected, and the frontier condition holds, so goodness is not implied by the other conditions.
- *Schubert examples.* The equivariance argument is right and elementary. Write φ(k, y) = (k, ky). Base change for the product K × Y gives a^* Rj_* E ≃ p^* Rj_* E. Local sections of the orbit map K → K·y give local constancy along the orbit. It needs the finite-rank step of gap 13. The strata are affine spaces or affine-space bundles over G/P, which are simply connected.
- *D-module goodness.* Over C it agrees with the sheaf notion: regular lisse modules on T are exactly the local systems on T(C) under Riemann–Hilbert, and gap 15 gives the compatibility with j_* and j_!. Over an arbitrary k, the argument for the Schubert strata needs gap 16.
- *Riemann–Hilbert.* The full equivalence first (regular holonomic complexes onto D^b_c(Y, C), with the listed compatibilities), then its restriction to the complexes lisse along a good S with t-exactness, is the standard order. The restriction to D^b_S as a category needs no goodness; t-exactness needs it, because the perverse t-structure on D^b_S does.
- *Conditions on maps.* *-pullback along a map that sends strata into strata preserves D^b_S for any stratifications. Proper pushforward needs the stronger local triviality. The parabola example for P¹ × A¹ → A¹ is right: the double cover x ↦ x² has fibre rank 2 off 0 and 1 at 0. The one-stratum example for the inclusion of the origin is right.
