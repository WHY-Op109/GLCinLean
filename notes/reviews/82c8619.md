1. **Mismatch: `Sheaves.qcoh_formalCompletion_localization` drops "quasi-separated" from the source's hypothesis** (`Sheaves/FormalCompletion.toml` line 6). The supporting texts are [GLC2] §C.5.6 (the presentation by pairs `I_small ⊆ I_big`, with `pr_small` through `X^{I_big} → X^{I_small} → Ran`, 28095–28116), the proof of [GaitsgoryAtiyahBott] Lemma 4.3.2 (the same argument for the big projection, 3200–3228), and §4.6.3 (the fibre is `(Ran)_{I/}`, a retract of `S × Ran`, 3388–3396). (b) "Pseudo-proper after every affine base change" does not match `Derived.PseudoProper`, which tests on base change by schemes of finite type. Say only "pseudo-proper (Derived.PseudoProper)".
11. **`Ran.pseudoProper_of_isProper` (wording).** `Derived.PseudoProper` is defined for classical prestacks, but `GLCinLean.Ran` is a prestack on derived test schemes. The old node said "Use classical test schemes"; the new one dropped that sentence. Say that the node applies the definition to the restriction of `Ran(X)` to classical test schemes. The words "derived nilpotents do not change D-modules" then cover the comparison.
12. **`Ran` (wording).** "For a prestack Y … with the diagonal maps and the disjointness and factorization diagrams": for an arbitrary prestack (for example `BG`) there is no disjoint locus. The disjointness and factorization diagrams need `Y = X` or `X_dR` for a separated scheme `X` ([GaitsgoryAtiyahBott] §4.1.1, `Tamagawa.tex` 2988: "Let X be a (separated) scheme").
13. **`Examples.exists_setting_rank_pos` (`deps` and wording).** The theorem is about `Statement.Setting`, but `Statement.Setting` is not in its `deps`. As `Setting` stands, its field `dualIdentification : RootDatumDuality G dualGroup` has the type of an opaque placeholder (`Statement/Conjecture.lean` 41–43, 69), so no element of `Setting` can be constructed until `RootDatumDuality` is replaced by `Groups.dualityData` (the replacement contract in `Statement/Conjecture.toml` line 4, which also adds a dual rank and pinning). The `desc` should name `Statement.Setting` and say that the theorem comes after that replacement. Otherwise it compares correctly with `Setting`: `P¹` is smooth, proper and connected, `O(−1)` is a theta characteristic, and the `rank` field is the torus rank.
14. **`Automorphic.halfTwistedEquivUntwisted` (wording, inherited).** "Compatible with the chosen root" and "it does not change the Hecke normalization" are not clear. [GLC1] (the remark after §1.1.5, 1410–1416) says that the twisted category must be used for the Hecke functors, since otherwise `Rep(Ǧ)` must be twisted by a `Z_Ǧ`-gerbe. A clearer sentence: "the Hecke action is defined on Dmod_½(Bun_G) and is not transported along this equivalence".
15. **`Hecke.ranSupersetPullback_fullyFaithful` (name).** It is generic Ran geometry but keeps the `Hecke.` namespace. Decision 7 of 2026-11-13 moved generic geometry out of `Hecke`, and its neighbour moved to `Ran.smallProjection_pseudoProper`.
