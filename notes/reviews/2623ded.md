### Notes on the verdicts

- **`Sheaves.indcohTStructure`.** Apart from point 1, the node is right. [IndCoh] Prop. 11.7.5 (9236–9256) characterizes the t-structure on a laft Artin stack by t-exactness of f^{IndCoh,*} for flat f : S → Y, with S a derived scheme almost of finite type, and part (a) gives compatibility with filtered colimits. Smooth maps also determine it, because the stack has a smooth atlas. The node now names the *-pullback, as the earlier gap asked.
- **`Ran`.** Apart from point 2, the node is right, and the new clause on separatedness matches [GaitsgoryAtiyahBott] §4.1.1 (2988). On classical test schemes, the S-points of the colimit of the X^I are the nonempty finite subsets of X(S), by the proof of Prop. 4.1.3 (3008–3017).
- **`Groups.iwahoriWeylLength`.** I checked the new sentences against Tau Ceti. `coweightLattice` is the set of points at which every root takes an integer value (`CoweightLattice.lean` 303–304). So over ℤ it is all of N, and over ℝ it is the real coweight lattice P∨, which contains the real subspace on which all roots vanish. `fundamentalAlcoveStabilizer` assumes only `CharZero`, `Finite`, `Nonempty` and `Preorder` (`Alcove.lean` 81). Its map goes to P∨/(P∨ ∩ Q∨) (151–160), and its kernel is Ω ∩ W_aff (172–176). The decomposition and the surjectivity (195, 208, 218) assume a field with an archimedean linear order and a crystallographic, reduced, irreducible pairing (188–190). Tau Ceti does not prove that Ω ∩ W_aff is trivial, which is the injectivity that the node lists as an obligation. The map N/Q∨ → P∨/Q∨ is injective because N is free, so "the restriction of Tau Ceti's map" is meaningful. Reading "W̃ ∩ `fundamentalAlcoveStabilizer`" needs the map from the group over ℤ to the group over ℝ; the node's words "elements whose extension to ℝ fixes the fundamental alcove" supply it.
- **`Ran.Representations.linearFunctorEquivComodule`.** The statement now matches [RaskinCPSI] Lemma 6.32.1 (`cpsi.txt` 3671–3680): for D a D(X^I)-module, the D(X^I)-linear functors Rep(Γ)_{X^I} → D are the O_{Γ,X^I}-comodules in D, through F ↦ F(O_{Γ,X^I}), and lax monoidal structures correspond to algebra structures. The added "compatible with the diagonal and factorization maps over Ran" is not in the lemma; it is a requirement of the plan, which is acceptable.
- **`Sheaves.properPerfectPushforward`.** The new sentence is right. For X a proper scheme, QCoh(X) is compactly generated, hence dualizable, so `Sheaves.externalProduct_isEquivalence_of_dualizable` gives QCoh(X × S) ≃ QCoh(X) ⊗ QCoh(S). Tensoring the adjunction p^* ⊣ Γ(X, −) of continuous functors with id_{QCoh(S)} gives the adjunction for the projection X × S → S, so f_* = Γ(X, −) ⊗ id. The node does not say why QCoh(X) is dualizable; this is standard.
- **`Stacks.exists_quotientStratification_of_isQCA`.** The node now has the quasi-compact embeddings of [DG2] Prop. 2.3.4 (2156) and the reason the devissage needs them (2097–2103). Its étale covers are the characteristic-zero case (ii) of the proposition (2168).
- **`Groups.splitReductiveGroup` and `Groups.chevalleyInvolution`.** The group now lives over ℤ and is base-changed, as [ConradRGS] p. 197 does ("the Existence Theorem over Z (from which the Existence Theorem is deduced in general via base change)"). This fixes the inconsistency with `Groups.langlandsDual`. `Groups.chevalleyInvolution` now names `Groups.splitReductiveGroup` as the provider of the isomorphism theorem. The claim θ^*V^μ ≅ V^{−w0 μ} holds, because θ fixes B and acts on characters by −w0.

1. **`DModules.hyperbolicRestriction` names the wrong second functor** (`DModules/Hyperbolic.toml` line 6). The node now says "The pull-pull functors (i^+)^•(p^+)^! and (i^-)^•(p^-)^*". The second functor of [BradenDG] §3.1.2 is (i^−)^! ∘ (p^−)^•, a !-pullback after a *-pullback (`braden.tex` 2445–2470, and Theorem 3.1.6, 2506–2513). The functor (i^-)^•(p^-)^* is two *-pullbacks. ### Reasons, node by node

#### The good stratification (`Constructible.DerivedCategory`, `Constructible.PerverseSheaves`, `DModules.perverseHeart`, `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence`)

- **The condition is the right one.** The node calls S good when, for every stratum T and every local system L of finite rank on T(C), the *- and !-extensions of L along T → Y are in D^b_S. Its !-half always holds: the stalks of j_!L are those of L on T and 0 elsewhere. So the condition says that j_{T*}L is S-constructible. This is the usual condition, and it gives what the nodes claim (standard mathematics; [BBD] §2.1 is not in the repository). D^b_S is generated by the j_{T!}L. D(j_{T!}L) = j_{T*}(L^∨)[2 dim T] is in D^b_S by goodness, so Verdier duality preserves D^b_S. Then j_T^! = D j_T^* D preserves it too. The perverse t-structure is glued from the strata, and the intermediate extensions stay in D^b_S.
- **The examples are right** (standard mathematics). For a Whitney stratification, j_{T*}L is constructible along the strata, by Thom's first isotopy lemma. An orbit stratification of a connected algebraic group H with finitely many orbits is Whitney. The orbits are smooth, connected and locally closed, and orbit closures are unions of orbits. For two orbits, the Whitney condition holds on a dense open part of the smaller orbit. That part is H-stable, so it is the whole orbit. On a finite Schubert union, L+G and I act through a quotient of finite type, so the Schubert cases are covered. [MV] §2 (`Satake.tex` 1020–1027) also says that the L+G-orbits are simply connected.
- **The umbrella counterexample is stated correctly.** I checked the computation of `82c8619.md` (part B, point 1). X = {x² = y²z}, with A the z-axis. Its singular locus is A, since the gradient (2x, −2yz, −y²) vanishes only for x = y = 0. U = X − A = X ∩ {y ≠ 0} ≅ C × C^*, by (x, y) ↦ (x, y, x²/y²). The normalization ν(y, t) = (yt, y, t²) is finite and birational, so IC_X = ν_*C[2]. Its stalk has rank 2 over A − 0 and rank 1 at 0. For the !-restriction of the constant sheaf, H¹(i^!C_X) has rank 1 over A − 0 and rank 0 at 0. The node says only "the !-restriction to the axis"; see gap 5.
- `Constructible.PerverseSheaves` (line 20) and `DModules.analyticDeRham_isEquivalence` (line 6) now ask for good S. For good S, the regular Riemann–Hilbert correspondence restricts to the full subcategories: DR(F) is in D^b_S exactly when F is lisse along S. It is t-exact for the perverse t-structure, which now exists. It commutes with the six operations on all regular holonomic complexes, so the claims about pullbacks, proper pushforward and external products hold (standard mathematics; [HottaTakeuchiTanisaki] Chapters 7–8 are not in the repository). The new dependency `DModules.shriekTensor` gives ⊠. Agree.

So the !-bounds work, and the doubt of `82c8619.md` (part B, point 3) is resolved.
