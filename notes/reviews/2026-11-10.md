### M1. Parity of Bott–Samelson pushforwards is stated for the wrong stratification (must fix)

- **What the plan says.**
  - `AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGrassmannian/BottSamelson.toml:11`
    (`Hecke.bottSamelsonPushforward_parity`) states: for simply connected G, the pushforward of
    the constant sheaf along each Bott–Samelson map of `Hecke.bottSamelsonResolution` is parity
    "for the L+G-orbit stratification". Its restrictions to every L+G-stratum are "constant".
  - `BottSamelson.toml:4` (`Hecke.bottSamelsonResolution`) builds these maps "for each
    I-Schubert variety in Gr_G", that is, for Iwahori-orbit closures.
  - `GeometricLanglands/Satake/Spherical.toml:18` (`Satake.icStalkParity_complex`), step (1),
    moves the closure of Gr^λ to the neutral component by left translation by a length-zero
    element ω of the Iwahori–Weyl group. Step (2) then applies the node above to the image.
- **What is wrong.**
  - ω normalizes the Iwahori subgroup I, but in general not L+G. So the translate of the
    closure of Gr^λ is an I-Schubert variety in the neutral component that is not L+G-stable.
  - Example: G = PGL_2 and λ = ω_1, minuscule, in the odd component. The closure of Gr^λ is a
    copy of P^1. Its translate is the closure of the one-dimensional I-orbit in Gr_{SL_2}. It
    meets the two-dimensional L+G-orbit Gr^{α∨} only in a line A^1. So the pushforward
    (here the constant sheaf on that P^1) restricted to Gr^{α∨} is not a local system. The
    node's conclusion is false for the varieties it is applied to.
  - JMW prove parity with respect to the P_I-orbits on G/P_J (the two situations at
    `paritysheaves_a.tex:2880–2887`). For a Bott–Samelson sequence starting at I_0 = ∅, the
    pushforward is parity for the Iwahori-orbit stratification, not for the L+G-orbits.
- **Required change.**
  - State `Hecke.bottSamelsonPushforward_parity` for the I-orbit stratification. Or restrict it
    to L+G-stable targets with I_0 the finite-type subset for L+G.
  - In `Satake.icStalkParity_complex`, add the missing step. IC_λ is L+G-equivariant, so its
    cohomology sheaves on each Gr^μ are constant local systems. Then parity along the I-orbits
    implies parity along the L+G-orbits. For the !-restriction this uses that i_C^! of a local
    system on the smooth Gr^μ is a shift by 2·codim C, which is even.
  - Replace "an isomorphism over Gr^λ" in step (2) by "an isomorphism over the open I-orbit".
- **The rest of the route is sound.** IC_λ[−d_λ] is a direct summand of f_* C_BS by the
  decomposition theorem, because f is birational and dim BS = d_λ. f_* C_BS is even: its stalks
  are fibre cohomologies paved by affine spaces, and it is self-dual up to [2d_λ]. A summand of an
  even complex is even. So the constructible degrees are ≡ d_λ mod 2, as the node claims.

### M2. The JMW locators reverse JMW's proof order (should fix)

- **What the plan says.** `BottSamelson.toml:11` proves the pushforward parity from "Proposition
  4.3, … Theorem 4.8, … Proposition 4.10, … Proposition 2.36". Its source field is
  "Propositions 2.36, 4.3, 4.10, Theorem 4.8 and the proof of Theorem 4.6".
- **What JMW says.** I numbered section 4 by hand from `\newtheorem{thm}{Theorem}[section]`
  (`paritysheaves_a.tex:241–255`):
  - Prop. 4.3 = `:2810` (strata: even cohomology, constant local systems). Right.
  - Thm. 4.6 = `:2913` (existence of parity sheaves). Thm. 4.8 = `:2947` (convolution preserves
    parity). Prop. 4.10 = `:2981` (quotient maps preserve parity). Right.
  - **Prop. 4.11 = `:3086`** (`prop:BSparity`): "f_* k_{BS} is parity". This is exactly the
    statement of the node. **It is not cited.**
  - Prop. 2.36 = `:1502` (proper even maps preserve parity). Right.
  - Rem. 2.17 = `:910`. Right.
- **What is wrong.** JMW prove Prop. 4.11 from Prop. 4.10 and proper base change
  (`:3089–3100`). They then prove Thm. 4.8 *from* Prop. 4.11 (`:3141–3160`). The plan lists
  Thm. 4.8 as an input to Bott–Samelson parity. If someone follows the plan literally, the
  route is circular.
- **Required change.** Cite Prop. 4.11 as the statement. Give the route as Prop. 4.3 (the
  assumptions (2.1) and (2.2)), Prop. 2.36 → Prop. 4.10, proper base change → Prop. 4.11. Drop
  Thm. 4.8, or mark it as a consequence.

### U1. The new name of the main theorem (unsure; owner's choice)

- The new name is `exists_isLanglandsFunctor_and_isEquivalence_of_isLanglandsFunctor`
  (`GLCinLean/Statement/Conjecture.lean:115`). The statement is
  `(∃ F, IsLanglandsFunctor S F) ∧ ∀ F, IsLanglandsFunctor S F → F.IsEquivalence`.
- Read as `exists_isLanglandsFunctor` + `and` + `isEquivalence_of_isLanglandsFunctor`, the name
  spells the conclusion exactly. The old name could be misread as ∃ F, (IsLanglandsFunctor F ∧
  IsEquivalence F), which is weaker. The new name removes that misreading.
- But in Mathlib, `A_and_B_of_C` normally means C → (A ∧ B). The theorem has no hypothesis, so
  a reader may look for one. The blind reader independently called the name misleading, with
  about 80% confidence.
- A possible alternative is
  `exists_isLanglandsFunctor_and_forall_isLanglandsFunctor_imp_isEquivalence`. It is longer
  but has only one reading. I do not require the change.
