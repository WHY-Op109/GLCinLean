**U1: the report’s caution is justified, and the current wording resolves it.** GLC I §0.1.1 explicitly specifies characteristic zero; §0.5.7 says its conventions “largely follow” AGKRRV, whose §0.9.1 assumes algebraic closedness. That does not justify confidently asserting either that algebraic closedness is absent from all of GLC I’s hypotheses or that every convention is imported without exception. The branch now distinguishes the explicit wording from this project’s scope. That is accurate and sufficient; no Lean hypothesis needs changing. [GLC I](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.03599v3), [AGKRRV §0.9.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.01906v2)

**U2: I agree with the limitation of the argument, but would distinguish “not proved” from “false.”** The report establishes an equivalence of underlying ∞-functors. It does not establish that the resulting natural equivalence respects the specified coherent `k`-linear structures. Saying that mapping spaces do not explicitly carry that structure explains the missing step; it is not, by itself, a counterexample to recovering it in this particular situation.

The underlying comparison is sound. Write \(B(E)=E\star P\), \(L=\mathbb L_{G,\mathrm{coarse}}\), and \(T=\Psi F\). Spectral factorization and the adjunction \(B\dashv L\) turn the proposed condition into
\[
\operatorname{Maps}(\operatorname{Loc}V,TM)
\simeq
\operatorname{Maps}(\operatorname{Loc}V,LM).
\]
Joint naturality, the adjunction for `Loc`, and Yoneda give
\(\operatorname{Loc}^{R}T\simeq\operatorname{Loc}^{R}L\).
Full faithfulness of \(\operatorname{Loc}^{R}\) then gives \(T\simeq L\). Thus the comparison genuinely uses spectral decomposition; removing it from the statement’s definitions does not remove it from the justification of faithfulness. [GLC I §§1.2–1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.03599v3)

**The report’s argument that U2 does not alter the final conjecture is also convincing.** On compact objects, both a candidate \(F\) and the paper’s \(\mathbb L_G\) land in the bounded-below part, where \(\Psi\) is fully faithful. Their coarsenings therefore determine naturally equivalent restrictions. Compact generation and preservation of colimits extend that equivalence to the whole source. Consequently, every candidate has the same underlying equivalence status as \(\mathbb L_G\). Since each candidate is already `k`-linear, an underlying equivalence is an equivalence in `DGCat`; this does not require the comparison with \(\mathbb L_G\) itself to be `k`-linear. [GLC I §1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.03599v3)

- Retain an explicit future obligation for the **whole-statement comparison**, including compact-generation uniqueness and detection of equivalences in `DGCat`. The planned `isLanglandsFunctor_iff_comp_psi_iso_coarse` covers only the preceding comparison.
