## For the owner: mismatches and unsure verdicts

- **Mismatch (item 1, `Spectral.localization`, a missing step).** `Sheaves.passable_tensorBaseChange` with `Y = pt` identifies `QCoh(LS_H × (X^I)_dR)` with `QCoh(LS_H) ⊗ QCoh((X^I)_dR)`. That is the tensor product with *left* crystals. In the plan, `Dmod(X^I)` is *right* crystals, `IndCoh((X^I)_dR)` (`DModules.rightCrystals`). The node also gets dualizability from `DModules.qcaDMod_compactlyGenerated`, and it gets homology from p_!. Both are statements about right crystals. So the construction needs the left-to-right crystal equivalence `Υ : QCoh(Y_dR) ≃ IndCoh(Y_dR)` for Y locally almost of finite type ([Crystals] Prop. 2.4.4). It also needs the fact that Υ takes `*`-pullback to `!`-pullback. Only then do `(id × p)^*` and `id ⊗ p^!` match. No node in `plans/` states this. `Upsilon` appears only in `plans-proof/`, for infinite type.
- **Mismatch (item 1, wrong node for homology along X^I).** The node applies "id ⊗ H(X^I, −)" and cites `DModules.compactSupportedHomology`. That node (`plans/GLCinLean/AlgebraicGeometry/DModules/Homology.toml` line 6) defines one object, `H(Y) = p_!(ω_Y)`, with its trace and its functoriality. It does not define the functor `p_! : Dmod(X^I) → Vect` on all D-modules. The functor that the construction needs is `DModules.pseudoProperShriek`, or `DModules.deRhamPushforward` with its proper adjunction, since X^I is proper. `pseudoProperShriek` is now in `plans/` and is reached through the deps, but the node names the wrong input.
- **Mismatch (item 3, source locator only).** The new source `[GLC2] Theorem 1.7.2 and §1.7.3` gets Thm. 1.7.2 right. But §1.7.3 is a Remark (`Loc.tex` 2552–2558) that only names naive Satake and points to its definition. Naive Satake is defined at §1.7.6 (`Loc.tex` 2578–2582, label `sss:naive Sat`). The previous review proposed "§1.7.3", so this error came from that review. Suggested source: `[GLC2] Theorem 1.7.2 and §1.7.6; [GLC1] §1.2.1`.
- **Unsure (item 2, consistency of `Hecke.ranPseudoProper` with the new `Hecke.ranSpace`).** `Hecke.ranSpace` now says that Ran is the de Rham Ran, `Ran ≅ colim_I (X^I)_dR`. That matches the paper. But `Hecke.ranPseudoProper` (`plans/GLCinLean/AlgebraicGeometry/Ran/Homology.toml` line 6) still says "prove Ran(X) is pseudo-proper from its colimit over … finite powers X^I". With the new `ranSpace`, Ran is not the colimit of the X^I; it is the colimit of the (X^I)_dR. The same line goes on to "de Rham restriction identifies this presentation with the Ran prestack used for right crystals". This gives a correct reading: prove that colim X^I is pseudo-proper as in [GaitsgoryAtiyahBott], then use `(colim_I X^I)_dR ≅ Hecke.ranSpace`, and D-modules do not see the difference. But the text does not name `Hecke.ranSpace` as the target of that identification, and "the Ran prestack used for right crystals" is now ambiguous. Suggested fix: rewrite line 6 so that it names both prestacks and the isomorphism `(colim_I X^I)_dR ≅ Hecke.ranSpace`.

The other verdicts are *agree*, or *agree* with minor remarks.
